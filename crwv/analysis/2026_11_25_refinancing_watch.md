## Read
The company is refinancing below 15%, but only **secured**, contract-backed (Meta AA credit) debt at about 7–7.5% (if it prices). **Unsecured** pricing is about 16.4%, so the answer to the watch question is "yes via secured, no unsecured". The unsecured bonds are a market signal rather than a funding route, since the company says it won't issue more. Equity exposure runs through that secured funding staying available and through the ATM dilution. Price: CRWV about **$42.61** (Yahoo, 25 Nov 2026). No trade view.

**Next:** watch for the 8-K and pricing of the ~$4B facility; the 2032 yield drifting back under 15% would be the thaw signal. Next quarterly check Feb 2027, or earlier on a deal.
