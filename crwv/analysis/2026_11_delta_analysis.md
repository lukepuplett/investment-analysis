# CoreWeave (CRWV) – Delta Analysis: Q3 2026 vs prior assessment (November 2026)

Compares the Q3 2026 results (released 10 Nov 2026) with the last full analysis set (15 Apr 2026, built on Q4 2025 and the Q1 2026 print) and, where the repo has them, Q2 2026 and the October credit-scare notes. Sources are the 8-K and Exhibit 99.1, the presentation and the call, filed in [../quarterly/](../quarterly/) as `2026_Q3_*`; figures are checked in [2026_11_q3_results_summary.md](2026_11_q3_results_summary.md). The Q3 10-Q was not on EDGAR when this was written, so debt and lease schedules, convertible terms and a fully diluted share count are open. Amounts are unaudited, in $ millions unless noted. Related: [2026_04_15_investment_thesis.md](2026_04_15_investment_thesis.md), [2026_04_15_risk_assessment.md](2026_04_15_risk_assessment.md), [2026_04_15_valuation_analysis.md](2026_04_15_valuation_analysis.md), and the toy residual model in [../model/](../model/output.md).

## Executive Summary

**BLUF: Demand and operations beat; the funding picture is the part that changed for the worse. Revenue ($3,661m) and adjusted operating income ($379m, 10% margin) beat the company's own August outlook, and backlog rose to about $132.5bn. But capex guidance went up (FY26 $37–39bn), debt plus leases are about $66bn against $4.0bn of book equity, and the Q4 interest guide ($1.05–1.15bn) is larger than the implied Q4 adjusted operating income (about $750–850m). The April thesis assumed capex would moderate and financing was "secured" with moderate confidence; neither has played out as assumed. This is a research update, not a recommendation: the equity claim is a thin, highly levered residual that depends on funding, replacement capex and the multiple a buyer pays.**

## Operational changes

- **Power:** active power over 1.7 GW (added more than 200 MW in the quarter, doubled since the start of the year); contracted power over 4.6 GW (+900 MW); international contracted power above 1.3 GW, including India, Indonesia and Spain. Q1 baseline: 1.1 GW active, 3.5 GW contracted.
- **Backlog tenor (slide 13 chart):** 36% under 24 months (about $47.7bn), 38% in 25–48 months, 26% beyond 48 months. A year earlier it was 40% / 39% / 21%. The mix has moved towards longer contracts. More than 50% sits on contracts whose delivery commences by end-2026 (commencement, not recognition). Backlog is RPO plus other committed amounts, subject to delivery and availability; it is not a revenue floor.
- **Customers (call, a claim):** more than 75% of committed 2027 capex is tied to investment-grade and enterprise customers; the rest to AI labs and AI-native firms.
- **Prepayments:** collected on 70% of deals this year; about $9bn of prepayments and deposits expected in the next three months, over $3bn from recontracting existing infrastructure at premiums.
- **Pricing (call, not contracted):** July SKU prices up about 25% then another 10%; Ampere and Hopper up 30–50% quarter on quarter. About 130,000 GPUs come off contract in 2027 and 170,000 in 2028; management says 2027 renewals add approaching $4bn of annualised revenue with no new capex (about $10bn including 2028).

## Strategic and capital-structure updates

- About $4.2bn raised through an upsized convertible senior notes offering (terms not in the pack); capped-call purchases were $566m in Q3 and $1,058m in nine months.
- New at-the-market programme for up to 35 million Class A shares (no take-up disclosed). Private placements raised $2,982m in the nine months.
- $2.6bn DDTL 5.5 closed, the first facility for shorter-duration contracts. A new investment-grade DDTL was flagged for the coming weeks (call: implied 7–7.5% yield, financing over 90% of the capex).
- Management will not use unsecured debt at current yields and does not intend to issue more parent-level unsecured debt through 2027; it expects under $5bn of incremental parent-level capital through 2027. Nearly $5bn of undrawn committed DDTLs; over $9bn of required infrastructure already in CIP.
- Weighted average cost of debt 7.9% (down 250bp YoY; a claim). The Q4 interest guide implies a higher effective rate on existing debt, about 9.2% in the toy model, which is an inference from the guide and not disclosed.

## Valuation shifts

- **Price:** $48.73 official close on 10 Nov (prior close $48.98), against about $92 a week before 14 Oct, $65.78 on 14 Oct, and a spike to $53.00 after hours that faded to about $47.60. Primary-source bond prices are not in the pack; the mid-October report of about 15% yield on the unsecured 2032s is unverified.
- **Reference multiples (placeholder share count of 559m weighted average, not fully diluted):** market value about $27.2bn; adding debt, finance leases and cash gives enterprise value (excluding operating leases, since lease cost is already inside EBITDA) of about $68bn, or about 7.6x Q3 annualised adjusted EBITDA ($9.0bn) and 3.3x the exit-2026 run-rate revenue midpoint ($20.5bn). Treat as orientation only.
- **April framework:** the 2028 valuation target of $150B in the April thesis assumed 10–12x sales on profitability; with a roughly $27bn market value on a basic count, that pillar needs a fresh look rather than a restatement here.
- The toy model gives no price target. Its end-2028 residual range per share, by EV/EBITDA of 4/6/8x, is about $10/$96/$182 for the committed-backlog path, with steady-state owner earnings negative; see [../model/output.md](../model/output.md).

## Competitive landscape

Nothing new in the pack beyond the pricing colour above. Capacity is still the limit on short-term revenue ("constrained primarily by the capacity allocated"). The October selloff spread to other neoclouds and Oracle, which suggests the funding risk is sector-wide, not CoreWeave-specific (reported, not checked).

## Management commentary changes

- **Tone:** more confident on demand and pricing, more defensive on funding: explicit refusal to issue unsecured debt at current yields, and a repeated claim that contracted cash flow is sufficient to service and repay today's debt plus planned additions, before renewals. The toy model tests that claim; it is not accepted here.
- **New claims to track:** EBITDA less interest (excluding prepayments) to more than double in 2027; adjusted net loss to shrink meaningfully in 2027 and turn positive in the first half of 2028 and for full-year 2028; 2027 capex in line with 2026 if only the committed backlog is delivered.
- **Capital allocation:** DDTLs first, prepayments second, OEM financing for short-term capacity, limited parent raise.

## What would change this assessment

- **Q3 10-Q:** instrument-level debt, coupons, maturities, covenants; convert and capped-call terms; fully diluted shares; depreciation policy.
- **Primary-source bond prices** and any new IG DDTL pricing.
- **Backlog conversion by year in dollars** and customer concentration.
- **Used-GPU values and fleet life**, which decide replacement capex and the terminal value.
- **Whether prepayments keep arriving** at the 2026 pace, and whether 2027 renewals price as management says.
