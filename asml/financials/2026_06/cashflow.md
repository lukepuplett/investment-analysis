## Key Notes
- **Buyback pace slowed meaningfully** — H1 2026 treasury share purchases of €2,077.5M are roughly half of H1 2025's €4,077.8M. Combined with a €693.2M debt repayment (vs. near-zero in H1 2025), this suggests ASML shifted capital allocation somewhat from buybacks toward debt reduction this half — worth understanding the rationale (deleveraging ahead of a capex cycle? balance sheet conservatism?) in the next full analysis.
- Dividend payments grew (+26.2% H1 YoY) consistent with rising earnings.
- The Q1-weighted working-capital drag (see Operating Activities note above) is the standout item for follow-up — Q2 alone shows healthy cash conversion (€1,703.0M operating cash flow against €2,917.6M net income), so this looks more like timing than a structural change, but should be confirmed.
