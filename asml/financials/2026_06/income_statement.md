## Key Notes
- **Strong reacceleration:** revenue growth (+21.3% YoY Q2) and operating margin expansion (+250bps) together drove net income up 27.4% YoY — a clean beat-and-scale quarter with no unusual/one-time items distorting the picture (unlike GLW, HOOD, or INTC this cycle).
- Lithography system unit sales up 19.7% YoY (76 → 91 units) — a real unit-volume confirmation behind the revenue growth, not just pricing/mix.
- Diluted share count fell slightly (-0.9% YoY) on continued buybacks, adding a modest extra boost to per-share growth versus net income growth.
