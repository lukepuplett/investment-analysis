## Key Notes — Flag for Follow-Up
- **Accounts receivable more than doubled in six months (€3,023M → €7,252.6M, +140%).** This is the single most notable balance sheet item this quarter and directly explains why operating cash flow went negative for H1 2026 despite record net income (see Cash Flow statement). Likely explanation: large lithography systems (especially High-NA EUV, ASML's highest-value product line) recognized as sales near quarter-end with billing/collection lagging — common in ASML's business given large single-system deal sizes — but this should be confirmed against ASML's own commentary/investor call before treating it as routine. Worth flagging in the next full risk assessment as a collections/working-capital item to monitor into Q3.
- **Cash fell sharply** (€12,916.0M → €6,671.9M, -48%) — driven by the AR build plus continued heavy shareholder returns (dividends + buybacks, see Cash Flow statement) and €693.2M of debt repayment in H1.
- Inventory grew modestly (+2.7%) — in line with revenue growth, no unusual buildup signal.
- Equity strengthened (+11.3% in six months) despite the cash drawdown, reflecting strong retained earnings from record net income.
