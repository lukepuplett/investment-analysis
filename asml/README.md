# ASML Holding N.V. (ASML)

## Company Overview

ASML is the world's leading supplier of photolithography systems for the semiconductor industry, and the sole global supplier of EUV (extreme ultraviolet) lithography machines — a critical bottleneck technology for leading-edge chip manufacturing (TSMC, Samsung, Intel, Micron, SK Hynix are customers).

**Ticker:** ASML
**Exchange:** NASDAQ (ADR) / Euronext Amsterdam (primary)
**Sector:** Information Technology
**Industry:** Semiconductor Equipment
**Reporting:** Dutch foreign private issuer — files 20-F (annual) and 6-K (quarterly interim reports) with the SEC, not 10-K/10-Q. Reports in EUR.

## News Update - 07/15/2026: Q2 2026 6-K — Strong Reacceleration, One Working-Capital Item to Watch

- **Q2 2026 (US GAAP):** Total net sales €9,326.5M (+21.3% YoY); operating income €3,456.1M (+29.7% YoY, margin 37.1%, +250bps); net income €2,917.6M (+27.4% YoY); diluted EPS €7.58 (+28.5% YoY).
- **H1 2026:** Net sales €18,093.4M (+17.2% YoY); net income €5,674.3M (+22.2% YoY); diluted EPS €14.73 (+23.8% YoY).
- **Volume confirmation:** Lithography system unit sales up 19.7% YoY (76 → 91 units) — the growth is backed by real unit volume, not just price/mix.
- **Balance sheet flag:** Accounts receivable more than doubled in six months (€3,023M → €7,252.6M) — this, combined with a large working-capital swing, pushed H1 2026 operating cash flow **negative** (€(482.5)M) despite record net income. Q2 alone was healthily cash-generative (€1,703.0M operating CF), so this looks like a Q1-weighted timing effect (likely large-system billing/collection lag, common for ASML's high-value EUV systems) rather than a structural issue — but should be confirmed against Q3 results before being dismissed.
- **Capital allocation shift:** Buyback pace roughly halved YoY (€2,077.5M H1 2026 vs. €4,077.8M H1 2025) while ASML repaid €693.2M of debt in H1 (vs. near-zero H1 2025) — a modestly more conservative capital allocation posture this half.
