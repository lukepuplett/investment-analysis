## GAAP-to-Non-GAAP Reconciliation Context (from Q2 FY2027 earnings call, 9/1/2026)

Management reported **Non-GAAP operating income of $42.6M (≈15% margin)** for Q2 FY2027, up from $39.6M a year ago — a sharp contrast to the **GAAP operating loss of ($56.9M)** shown above. The gap is driven primarily by:

- **Restructuring charges of ~$23.3M** incurred in Q2, tied to a company-wide restructuring decision made at the start of the quarter (per CEO Bill Staples, on the call).
- **Stock-based compensation** ($75.0M in Q2, +38.2% YoY — see table above), which is excluded from Non-GAAP opex.
- **JiHu-related expenses** (~$3.0M in Q2, flat YoY; full-year JiHu expense guided to ~$50M vs. $13M last year).

Excluding these items, underlying operating leverage in Q2 was *positive* YoY, not negative — the GAAP loss widening reflects one-time/non-cash items layered on top of continued (and by some measures accelerating) topline execution:

- **Net ARR grew 42% YoY**, the second-highest growth quarter in four years, on the largest gross bookings quarter in company history.
- **Dollar-based net retention (DBNR) was 117%,** up sequentially for the first time since 2024.
- **Deals ≥$500K grew >150% YoY**; first orders grew >100% YoY (~1,700 in the quarter).
- Guidance was **raised**, not cut: FY2027 revenue now guided to $1.129B–$1.133B (+18–19% YoY), Non-GAAP operating income to $148M–$152M.

**Flex launch (new in Q2) will affect future revenue recognition timing:** GitLab's new consumption-based commercial model shifts some self-managed license revenue (previously ~15% recognized upfront) to ratable recognition over the contract term. Management estimates a **maximum ~$13M FY2027 revenue timing headwind** (≈$5M per $50M of self-managed renewals converting to Flex), with no change to underlying cash economics or customer commitments. Flex commitments are included in Total RPO but excluded from Current RPO, so **Total RPO and CRPO growth rates may diverge going forward** — flagged as a three-point CRPO headwind this quarter. This is a structural reporting change to track each quarter, not a one-time item.

## Notable Items

- **Q2 GAAP operating/net loss widening is largely explained by restructuring charges (~$23.3M) and elevated SBC, not underlying deterioration.** Non-GAAP operating income was positive at $42.6M (15% margin), and bookings/retention metrics (42% net ARR growth, 117% DBNR, record gross bookings) point to an inflection in underlying business health, not weakness. GAAP figures above remain the correct basis for this repo's standard tables, but should be read alongside this non-GAAP/bookings context.
- **Gross margin compression accelerated:** 87.9% → 84.1% in Q2 (vs. 88.3% → 85.8% in Q1), with cost of revenue growing 60% YoY against 21% revenue growth. Per the earnings call, this is attributed mainly to SaaS/Dedicated mix shift (34% of revenue, +36% YoY) rather than AI inference costs — management noted Duo Agent Platform's cloud-agnostic/model-agnostic design keeps inference costs largely outside the GitLab margin structure for many customers.
- **G&A growth (+52.5% YoY in Q2) is a standout outlier** relative to prior-quarter trend (G&A was roughly flat YoY in Q1); likely includes restructuring-related charges — confirm exact allocation in the 10-Q's restructuring footnote before treating as a run-rate change.
- **H1 net loss narrower YoY** (($41.8M) vs ($45.1M)) only because Q1 outperformance offset Q2's GAAP deterioration — the H1 "improvement" masks a negative in-quarter GAAP trend, though non-GAAP/bookings trends were strong in both quarters.
- **SBC growing faster than revenue in Q2** (+38.2% YoY vs. +21.3% revenue growth), reversing the favorable trend seen in Q1 — dilution-adjusted earnings quality moved the wrong direction this quarter.
- **Flex is a new structural variable for FY2027/FY2028 modeling** — watch quarterly disclosures on Flex adoption ($ committed, customer count) and the associated revenue-recognition/RPO timing effects; see reconciliation section above.
