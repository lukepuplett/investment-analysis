## Key Notes

- **Operating cash flow declined 6.1% YoY on a six-month basis** ($146.1M vs. $155.7M) — a reversal from Q1's standalone 40.4% YoY OCF growth. This is consistent with the profitability deterioration seen in the Q2 income statement (operating loss widened sharply in the June–July quarter) and should be reconciled together in the next full analysis.
- **Financing activities used $143.3M in H1**, a large swing from $15.7M provided a year ago. Combined with the $65M stockholders' equity decline noted in the balance sheet, this points to meaningful cash returned to shareholders or debt/lease repayments during the period — needs verification against the financing activities detail in the full 10-Q (e.g., share repurchases, tax withholding on equity award settlements).
- **Investing activities used far less cash** than a year ago (($5.4M) vs. ($138.5M)) — likely reflects reduced net purchases of short-term investments (treasury management), not a change in core capex needs (capex remains minimal at $2.6M, under 0.5% of H1 revenue).
- **Free cash flow of $143.5M for H1**, down modestly (-5.5% YoY) — still solidly positive and a high FCF margin (~26% of H1 revenue), but the deceleration versus Q1's standalone trend is the item to watch heading into Q3.

*All values in millions USD unless noted. FCF = Free Cash Flow (Operating Cash Flow − Capital Expenditures).*
