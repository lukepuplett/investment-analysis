## Notable Items

- **Operating leverage continues to build:** Operating loss narrowed from ($34.6M) to ($15.7M) YoY — a 54.5% improvement — despite gross margin compression. Operating expenses grew only 8.2% while revenue grew 23.1%, showing clear cost discipline.
- **Gross margin compression (88.3% → 85.8%):** Cost of revenue grew nearly 50% YoY, outpacing revenue growth (23.1%). Worth monitoring whether this reflects infrastructure/hosting cost investment (e.g., AI features, Duo) or mix shift.
- **SBC declining as % of revenue (26.0% → 19.0%):** A meaningful improvement in dilution-adjusted earnings quality; SBC dollar amount fell YoY even as revenue grew.
- **Net loss narrowed 86% YoY** to just ($5.0M) — GitLab is within striking distance of GAAP net income breakeven.
