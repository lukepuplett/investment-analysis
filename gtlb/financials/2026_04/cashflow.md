## Key Notes

- **Operating cash flow grew 40.4% YoY** to $149.2M — outpacing revenue growth (23.1%) and confirming continued operating leverage and cash conversion strength.
- **Investing activities turned cash-positive** ($4.2M provided vs. $81.9M used a year ago) — likely reflects net maturities/sales of short-term investments exceeding purchases this quarter, a treasury management effect rather than a change in core capex needs (capex itself remains minimal at $2.4M, ~0.9% of revenue).
- **Financing activities used $47.8M**, a swing from a $3.3M source a year ago — worth checking the 10-Q financing detail (e.g., share repurchases, tax withholding on equity awards, debt repayments) in the next full analysis cycle to confirm the driver.
- **Free cash flow of $146.8M** (39.3% YoY growth) continues to validate GitLab's asset-light SaaS cash generation profile.

*All values in millions USD unless noted. FCF = Free Cash Flow (Operating Cash Flow − Capital Expenditures).*
