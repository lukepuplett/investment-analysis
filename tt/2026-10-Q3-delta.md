## Numbers ($m unless stated, Q3-26 vs Q3-25)
- Bookings **7,248 (+21%; organic +20%)**; book-to-bill 107%. **Backlog $12.5bn (+73%)**, Americas Commercial HVAC backlog up ~80%.
- Revenue **6,760 (+18%; organic +16%)**.
- Adjusted operating income 1,364 (+15%); **adjusted op margin 20.2% (−40bp)**; GAAP op margin 19.9% (−40bp). Adjusted EBITDA 1,447 (21.4%, −40bp).
- **Adjusted EPS $4.86 vs $3.88 (+25%)**; GAAP continuing EPS $4.79 (+25%).
- Segments: Americas revenue 5,648 (+21%, organic +19%), adj margin 22.3% (+50bp); EMEA revenue 756 (+1%) but bookings +40% (book-to-bill 147%), adj margin 17.2% (−250bp); Asia Pacific revenue 356 (+8%), adj margin 18.6% (−390bp).
- YTD OCF 3,060; **YTD FCF 2,828 (+~60%)**. Debt 4,218; cash 1,227.
- YTD through October: ~$3.4bn deployed or committed (dividends ~$930m, M&A $1,080m, buybacks $1.4bn).

## FY2026 guidance
- **Revenue raised:** reported growth ~13.5% (was ~11.5%), organic ~11% (was ~9%).
- **EPS reaffirmed, not raised:** adjusted $15.20–15.30; GAAP $15.00–15.10. Management cites "supply chain and inflationary headwinds".

## Delta vs thesis (`analysis/2026_05_investment_thesis.md`, BUY 7.5/10)
- Demand pillar (data-centre/commercial HVAC) strongly validated: record backlog, +20% organic bookings, revenue reacceleration in H2 as the May doc expected.
- Margin-expansion pillar is **not** being delivered: consolidated margin −40bp, EMEA and APAC down sharply, and a revenue raise with flat EPS implies lower incremental margins. This was already flagged "at risk" in May; it is worse, not broken.
- **Not thesis-invalidating, but the margin pillar is wobbling.** How to weigh it is Luke's call.
