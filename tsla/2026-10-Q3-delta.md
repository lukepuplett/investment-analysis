**Quality of the FCF:** working capital contributed about **+$2.6B** to OCF in Q3 (payables and accruals +$3,991M, deferred revenue +$1,255M, against prepaid −$1,818M, receivables −$565M, inventory −$313M). Strip that out and OCF would be about $3.7B and FCF about −$1.1B, roughly the same as Q2. So the swing back to positive FCF is mostly timing, not a step-up in underlying cash generation. Capex is running at about 2× last year's rate (TTM $15.4B).

Balance sheet: PP&E up $3.3B QoQ to $50.6B; other non-current assets up $3.1B to $12.6B (includes the SpaceX stake). Debt is still almost all non-recourse ($9,265M; recourse $2M). Separately, an 8-K filed 2026-09-29 shows Tesla replaced its $5B Citi revolver with new Wells Fargo-agented facilities (including a $2.0B 364-day revolver and a term loan); nothing was drawn on the old revolver.

## FSD, Robotaxi, Optimus (deck narrative, no audited numbers)

- **FSD (Supervised):** 1.74M active subscriptions (+18% QoQ, +67% YoY). Over 75% of new North America deliveries included FSD. Approved in 4 more EU countries since Q2 (Croatia, Slovenia, Czech Republic, Slovakia), so 9 in total, used by more than 100,000 customers. The cumulative supervised-FSD chart reads roughly 15B miles at Sep-2026 (read off a chart, approximate).
- **Robotaxi:** Cybercab started carrying riders in Austin on 3 Sep. Cumulative **unsupervised** miles were "nearly two million" at quarter-end, up eightfold QoQ. Austin hours are now 6am to midnight. Cybercab capacity is listed at >125k/yr in Texas, status "Production". No fleet size, intervention rate, or remote-ops cost was disclosed.
- **Optimus:** production has begun on Fremont high-volume lines, described as the "first production-quality bots"; Texas Optimus capacity is under construction. No unit volumes or revenue.
- **SI compute:** Cortex 1 (>105 MW) and Cortex 2 (>120 MW) are in production, with a target of 400 MW by year-end. Austin semiconductor fab commissioning is planned for 2027.

Merger talk in the diary (Barclays 16 Oct; ARK 21 Oct) remains sell-side and press commentary, not a company disclosure. Check the 10-Q subsequent-events note and any 8-K Item 1.01 or 8.01 filings.

## Against ROBOTAXI_ANALYSIS.md: does anything break the thesis?

**No. The thesis stands and is not rewritten.** Points against the quarterly checklist:

- *Cumulative safety miles (path to 5B by end-2028):* the doc doesn't say whether the 5B miles are supervised or unsupervised. Supervised FSD is already about 15B, but unsupervised is about 2M, three orders of magnitude short. At 8× QoQ growth it could close fast, but that rate won't hold. **Flag:** the metric definition should be pinned down in the next model refresh.
- *Intervention rate, remote-ops cost, Tier 3 approvals:* still not disclosed. No checklist item can be ticked from the filing.
- *Escalation triggers:* none hit. No fatality or incident was disclosed, and there's no evidence of a stall.
- *Core-auto context:* the doc's May valuation anchor ($398.87) predates this print. The core business looks steadier (auto GM ex-credits 18.1%), but operating margin is still compressed by R&D and SBC, and the positive FCF relies on working capital. Neither changes the robotaxi option math.
