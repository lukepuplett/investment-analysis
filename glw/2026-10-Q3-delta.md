## Numbers ($m unless stated, Q3-26 vs Q3-25)
- GAAP sales 4,855 (+18%); **core sales 5,081 (+19%)**, above the $4.9–5.0bn guide.
- **Core EPS $0.91 (+36%)**, above the $0.85–0.89 guide; GAAP EPS $0.78 (+56%).
- Core gross margin 40.5% (+150bp); core operating margin 22.7% (+310bp); core ROIC 16.7%. GAAP GM 37.3%, GAAP op margin 18.8%.
- **Optical Communications 2,379 (+44%)**, net income 532 (+80%); Enterprise Networks +84%, Gen AI product sales >+100% y/y.
- Glass Innovations 1,506 (−3%), NI 328 (−10%); Automotive 470 (+4%); **Solar 436 (+37%) but net loss (7)**, ramp "slower than anticipated"; Life Sciences & EGB 290, loss (28).
- Cash: OCF 1,060; capex 545 (vs 334); **adjusted FCF 661** (vs 535); 9M adjusted FCF 2,272 (vs 985).
- Four long-term, multibillion-dollar customer agreements signed in Q3, including Verizon and AT&T.

## Q4 2026 guidance
- Core sales ~+19% to $5.2–5.3bn; core EPS ~+36% to $0.96–1.00.
