## Numbers ($m unless stated, FQ4-26 vs FQ4-25, quarter ended 26 Sep 2026)
- Net sales **114,477 vs 102,466 (+12%)**, a September-quarter record. Products 82,904 (+12.5%); Services **31,573 (+9.8%)**, an all-time high.
- By category: **iPhone 57,583 (+17.5%)**, Mac 10,792 (+23.7%), iPad 6,229 (−10.4%), Wearables/Home/Accessories 8,300 (−7.9%).
- By region: Americas 48,680 (+10.2%), Europe 32,008 (+11.5%), **Greater China 17,539 (+21.0%)**, Japan 6,540 (−1.4%), Rest of APAC 9,710 (+15.0%).
- Gross margin **47.9%** (vs 47.2%), which Apple says includes about **1pt from tariff refunds**. Products GM 37.2% (36.2%); Services GM 76.0% (75.3%). So ex-refunds, GM was roughly flat.
- **R&D 11,730 (+32%)**; total opex +21%. Operating income 35,631 (+9.9%), so opex growth is eating most of the gross-profit gain.
- Net income 30,711 (+11.8%); **diluted EPS $2.10 vs $1.85 (+14%)**, incl. about $0.05 from tariff refunds. Tax rate 15.0% (16.3%). Diluted shares −1.6% y/y.
- Dividend $0.27/share (record 12 Nov, paid 16 Nov).

## Fiscal 2026 (full year)
- Revenue **478,834 (+15%)**; iPhone 254,098 (+21%); Services 123,301 (+13%); Greater China 82,378 (+28%). Diluted EPS $8.98 (+20%).
- OCF 145,097; capex 9,041, so **FCF ≈ 136bn (+38%)**. Buybacks 86.4bn, dividends 15.7bn.
- Net cash (cash + marketable securities less CP and term debt) about **56.8bn vs 33.8bn** a year ago; no new term debt issued in FY26.

## Things to watch
- **Inventories 14.5bn vs 5.7bn** a year ago (FY cash-flow line −8.9bn) and vendor non-trade receivables up 6.7bn. Likely a build for the iPhone Duo / iPhone 18 launch and holiday lineup, but worth confirming in the 10-K.
- **Intangible assets 20.3bn vs 11.1bn** (+9.2bn). Not explained in the release; check the 10-K for an acquisition or licence (possibly AI/Siri-related, unverified).
- R&D running +32% while Services growth slowed to under 10% in the quarter (FY +13%).
- Tariff refunds flattered both GM and EPS this quarter; strip them out when comparing to next quarter.
- New CEO John Ternus signs the release; CFO Kevan Parekh.
