# MRNA — Profitability and intismeran timing (chat notes, 23 Nov 2026)

Brief notes from a conversation with Luke. MRNA is still watchlist, not held.

## Is Moderna profitable?
- Recently only in Q3 2026 (Q3 2024 was a small profit, +$13M on $1.86B of revenue): net income $162M (GAAP EPS $0.38) vs a $200M loss in Q3 2025 ([Q3 8-K EX-99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1682852/000168285226000194/exhibit9912026q3pressrelea.htm)).
- Q3 is the seasonal autumn vaccine quarter. Q2 2026 was a $782M loss on $145M of revenue, so FY2026 is still a substantial loss.
- Net income by quarter ($M, from the 10-Q facts): Q3 2024 +13; Q1 2025 −971; Q2 2025 −825; Q3 2025 −200; Q1 2026 −1,343 (includes the settlement charge); Q2 2026 −782; Q3 2026 +162. Revenue ($M): 1,862; 108; 142; 1,016; 389; 145; 1,313. Q4 is not reported separately.
- Projection of income, free cash flow and balance sheet to 2030: [model/output.md](../model/output.md). In none of its three scenarios is 2028 cash break-even reached.
- Management targets cash break-even in 2028.

## When does the oncology drug (intismeran + Keytruda, with Merck) ship?
- BLA not yet filed; Moderna and Merck target filing by end-2026 and point to possible approval in H2 2027 ([Q3 call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/06/moderna-mrna-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/), [Jefferies, 18 Nov](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/moderna-at-jefferies-global-healthcare-conference-oncology-push-builds-93CH-5035533)).
- FDA mechanics: up to 60-day filing review, then 10-month standard review (6 months if priority). A late-December filing could push a decision into early 2028.
- First label would be adjuvant (resected high-risk) melanoma only; ~22,000 eligible US patients per Moderna. Marlborough site: one suite ready for melanoma launch, up to six more as demand appears.
- Lung, kidney and bladder indications come later, after their trials read out. Profits are shared with Merck.
- Meaningful oncology revenue for Moderna is therefore a 2028+ story.

## Can existing products make it profitable?
- Only partly. COVID keeps shrinking, RSV (mRESVIA) sales are soft, and mFLUSIVA got only a limited 2026–27 US launch because approval (Aug 2026) came after the season's contracting.
- Levers: cost and R&D cuts, a full 2027–28 flu season for mFLUSIVA, and US approval of the flu/COVID combo (approved in EU and Canada).
- These could get near cash break-even (2028 target), but durable profit likely needs intismeran or another new product.
- About $8.0B cash and investments at 30 Sep 2026, so no near-term need to raise money.
