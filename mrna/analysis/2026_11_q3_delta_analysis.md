## Pipeline / oncology deltas
- **Intismeran + Keytruda (INTerpath-001, resected high-risk melanoma):** full Phase 3 at ESMO (Oct). RFS/death HR 0.58 (−42%), DMFS/death HR 0.62 (−38%) vs Keytruda alone (ITT). Benefit larger after six months (piecewise HR 0.42, 95% CI 0.30–0.58). Confirms the 19 Aug topline. Economics shared with Merck. Overall survival still the open item.
- **mFLUSIVA (mRNA-1010 flu):** FDA approval in Aug (50–64; accelerated for 65+), fifth approved product; limited US launch this season because approval came after annual contracting. Also approved in Canada.
- **Flu/COVID combo (mRNA-1083):** approved in EU (mCOMBRIAX) and Canada (mCOFLU); under review in Japan, UK, Australia; still awaiting FDA guidance on US refiling.
- **Norovirus (mRNA-1403):** Phase 3 interim did not meet early-success criteria; no extra cohort this season. Negative.
- **Rare disease:** propionic acidemia registrational study fully enrolled, pivotal data expected in 2026; MMA pivotal decision deferred to that readout. mRNA-2808 oral at ASH, 12 Dec.
- **Analyst Day** was 12 Nov (materials not reviewed here).
