## Simple frameworks (order-of-magnitude)

### 1) Cash-adjusted
Cash+investments mid-year $6.9B → ~$6.0B after $950M settlement → still only a small fraction of market cap. Equity value is **almost all pipeline / platform NPV**, not net cash.

### 2) Sales multiple (trailing / near-term)
FY’25 revenue ~$1.94B → ~**37×** sales at $72B. Even if FY’26 grows 10% to ~$2.1B, still ~**34×**. Expensive for a loss-making vaccine company; only justifiable if oncology INT NPV is large and multi-indication.

### 3) Peak-COVID contrast
FY’22 revenue ~$18.9B / net income ~$8.4B — market once capitalized pandemic rents. Today’s cap is **below peak-era absolute profits** in a simple P/E sense but **far above** current earnings power. The bet is **new earnings power from oncology**, not a return to 2021–22 COVID volumes.
