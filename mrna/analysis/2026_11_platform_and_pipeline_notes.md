## Mechanism vs other RNA modalities (short)

mRNA therapeutics/vaccines deliver **temporary instructions** to produce a protein (or immunogen); the molecule is not intended to persist indefinitely. That is a different design goal than **gene silencing** modalities such as **siRNA or ASO** therapies, which typically reduce expression of a target transcript. The latter demonstrates RNA chemistry in medicine but is **not** automatic validation of Moderna’s LNP-mRNA vaccine/therapy economics or manufacturing stack.

**Oncology positioning:** Leading near-term use for INT is **adjuvant/therapeutic** neoantigen immunotherapy in defined cancer settings, not broad **population cancer prevention** (contrast infection-preventing vaccines such as HPV).

**What primary sources already establish ( headlines, not full labels):**

- Phase 3 **INTerpath-001** (resected melanoma): Merck/Moderna announced the trial **met RFS and DMFS vs KEYTRUDA alone** (topline **08/19/2026**). Overall survival was described as **immature** in that announcement; regulatory and benefit-risk conclusions are **not** implied here.
  - [Merck/Moderna INTerpath-001 press release](https://news.modernatx.com/merck-and-moderna-announce-phase-3-interpath-001-trial-of-intismeran-plus-keytruda-met-endpoints-of-rfs-and-dmfs-in-melanoma)
- Longer follow-up context may appear in meeting disclosures; treat any detailed effect sizes as **to-be-confirmed from primary slides/peer-reviewed publication**, not diary-grade facts in this repo.

**Evidence-stage caveats:** Topline success on intermediate endpoints does not equal approval, reimbursement, or durable long-term outcomes. Personalized therapy chains (sequencing → manufacturing → logistics) remain **execution- and cost-sensitive**.

**Economics / “ownership”:** INT economics are **split with Merck** under collaboration terms (development/profit share — see filings). Upside in a Moderna equity thesis is therefore **shared**, not fully captured by MRNA alone.

**Competition (uncertain / re-verify before comparing):** Other firms pursue individualized neoantigen approaches; relative standing versus competitors should be checked against **contemporaneous primary readouts** before asserting leadership or lag.

## Cross-cutting risks (additions to September themes)

- **Manufacturing / COGS:** Personalised therapies and large-scale seasonal vaccines impose **different** operational constraints; failure modes include batch failure, cold-chain complexity, and cost overruns.
- **Funding / competition:** Peers with deep vaccine franchises and oncology partnerships can **outspend or out-distribute** in overlapping markets; financing needs interact with litigation, settlement cash, and R&D depth.
- **Primary-source discipline:** SEC EDGAR pages may return **403** to some automated clients even when browsers succeed; when automation fails, **manually verify** or cite an alternate primary (company IR PDF, regulator DB) before stating dates or status.
