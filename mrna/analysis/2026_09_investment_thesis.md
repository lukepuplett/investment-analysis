# MRNA — Investment thesis (Sep 2026)

## One-liner
Moderna is transitioning from a post-pandemic vaccine cash-off to an **mRNA platform company** whose first Phase 3 oncology win (intismeran + KEYTRUDA in adjuvant melanoma, Aug 2026) re-opens a multi-year optionality narrative — already partly capitalized at ~$70B+ market cap.

## Bull case
1. **Platform validation:** First positive Ph3 for individualized neoantigen therapy and for an mRNA cancer medicine; expands addressable market beyond seasonal vaccines.
2. **Merck partnership:** Development/commercial muscle + 50/50 economics (subject to agreement terms) de-risks go-to-market vs going alone.
3. **Pipeline breadth:** Nine Ph2/3 INTerpath trials; additional wholly-owned oncology (e.g. mRNA-4359); rare disease (PA mRNA-3927); respiratory line extensions.
4. **Balance sheet:** Mid-2026 cash+investments $6.9B (pre-$950M settlement); YE guide $4.7–5.2B — runway to fund pivotal work without immediate dilutive crisis.
5. **Cost discipline:** OpEx outlook trimmed; manufacturing efficiencies cutting unused capacity costs.

## Bear case
1. **Price already moved:** ~$22 → ~$182 in the 52-week window; much of “first Ph3” celebration may be in the tape.
2. **Revenue still tiny vs valuation:** Q2’26 total revenue $145M; even if FY’26 grows ~10% off ~$1.9B FY’25, sales don’t support $70B+ without embedding large oncology NPV.
3. **Execution risk on personalized therapy:** Tumor sequencing → design → manufacture → dose chain is harder than vial vaccines; margins and capacity uncertain.
4. **Infectious-disease setbacks:** Norovirus Ph3 interim miss; COVID demand soft; combo vaccine FDA path unclear.
5. **Competition / standard of care:** Beating KEYTRUDA adjuvant bar is a high bar to keep clearing across tumors; rivals in IO and vaccines.

## Base-case framing for Luke
Treat MRNA as a **high-conviction watch / opportunistic starter**, sized like other speculative platform names (cf. early RKLB / biotech sleeve), not like MSFT/GOOG. The COVID→cancer re-rate thesis is **live and partly paid**. Edge now is **entry price and catalyst timing**, not discovering the story.

## Decision rules (working)
- **Add zone:** Prefer weakness that does not destroy the INT narrative (regulatory delays with intact data; vaccine soft patches).
- **Hold/trim zone:** Parabolic extension without new clinical/regulatory milestones.
- **Exit/cut:** Material INTerpath failure modes, or cash/opex trajectory that forces punitive financing into a weak tape.
