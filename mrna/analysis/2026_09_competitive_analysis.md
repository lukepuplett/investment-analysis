# MRNA — Competitive position (Sep 2026)

## Infectious disease vaccines
- **COVID:** Share pressure vs Pfizer/BioNTech and endemic demand decline; Moderna leaning on geographic contracts (e.g. UK partnership, Brazil supply collaboration) and next-gen (mNEXSPIKE approvals in JP/TW per Q2 update).
- **RSV:** mRESVIA — EU joint procurement (up to 24M doses across six countries cited in Q2 PR); AU/MX approvals noted.
- **Flu:** mFLUSIVA (mRNA-1010) — VRBPAC unanimous support; PDUFA **5 Aug 2026** (per Q2 PR). Competitors are entrenched egg/cell flu incumbents; mRNA flu must win on efficacy, strain match, and logistics.
- **Combos:** mCOMBRIAX EC authorization; US FDA refile guidance pending for flu+COVID — important for convenience-driven share.

## Oncology
- **Intismeran (mRNA-4157) + KEYTRUDA:** Differentiated as individualized neoantigen therapy. Ph3 adjuvant melanoma success vs KEYTRUDA alone is a high bar competitors must respect. Merck’s commercial oncology machine is a major advantage vs pure-play biotech go-it-alone.
- **Threats:** Other neoantigen / personalized vaccine efforts; next-gen IO combos; cell therapy; ADC expansions in overlapping tumors. Manufacturing personalization is both moat and bottleneck.

## Platform peers
- **BioNTech (BNTX):** Closest public mRNA platform peer; oncology-heavy narrative overlap.
- Traditional Big Pharma vaccine arms (GSK, Sanofi, Pfizer) remain distribution and tender competitors in respiratory.

## Moat hypothesis
True moat = **mRNA design + clinical experience + manufacturing scale + (for INT) personalized ops + Merck**. Not impregnable; time-limited IP and know-how. First Ph3 INT win is a **credibility moat** more than a permanent monopoly.
