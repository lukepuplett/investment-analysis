# Moderna (MRNA) — toy projection of income, free cash flow and balance sheet, FY2026E-FY2030E

*A toy, not a forecast and not a recommendation. FY2026 is from management's guidance and the Q3 10-Q; FY2027-30 are labelled assumptions in three scenarios. $ millions. Built 23 Nov 2026 from the Q3 2026 10-Q and the repo's Q3 delta note; the 2028 cash break-even target and the oncology timeline are from the repo's 23 Nov note and are not verified against the call.*

## Punchline

- **FY2026E** (guidance midpoint): revenue 2,333, operating loss -3,067, net loss -2,972, free cash flow about -2,711, year-end cash and investments 8,200.
- **Bear:** operating break-even not by 2030; free cash flow positive not by 2030; cumulative free cash flow 2027-30 -7,022; year-end 2030 cash and investments 578, lowest year-end 578.
- **Base:** operating break-even not by 2030; free cash flow positive not by 2030; cumulative free cash flow 2027-30 -3,433; year-end 2030 cash and investments 4,167, lowest year-end 4,167.
- **Bull:** operating break-even 2029; free cash flow positive 2029; cumulative free cash flow 2027-30 702; year-end 2030 cash and investments 8,302, lowest year-end 7,017.
- **Management's target is cash break-even in 2028.** None of the three scenarios reaches it. For free cash flow to be zero in 2028 on Base assumptions, revenue would need to be about 5.0bn with R&D at $2.4bn, 4.2bn at $2.0bn, or 3.4bn at $1.6bn (against about 2.3bn in 2026). The target therefore needs a big revenue step-up, deep cost cuts, or both.
- **Funding:** in Base cash ends 2030 at 4,167 and in Bull at 8,302. In **Bear** it falls to 578 at end-2030 after repaying the $600m term loan (cash was 2,860 a year earlier), close to the $500m minimum-cash covenant, which only applies if its market cap is below $5bn. The model adds no new financing, so Bear would in practice need to raise money, draw the delayed-draw loans or cut costs before then. The $3.0bn convertible falls due in Mar 2032, beyond the horizon.

## What would change the answer

- **Oncology timing and economics:** BLA filing by end-2026, approval date, label, Moderna's share of revenue and profit with Merck, and survival data.
- **Respiratory revenue:** COVID decline, RSV, a full flu season, US approval of the flu and COVID combination.
- **Cost cuts:** how much of the R&D reduction is sustained against the cancer trials and platform spending.
- **Receivables and Q4 cash:** the $1.2bn build in Q3 and its collection; the FY2026 cash guide is the anchor for the whole bridge.
- **Financing:** the $0.9bn of undrawn delayed-draw loans, a refinancing of the 2030 term loan, and what happens to the $3.0bn convertible with the stock at about its $210 conversion price.
- **Tax and interest:** loss carryforwards and the yield on a large cash pile.
