## BLUF

**WATCH / selective ACCUMULATE on pullbacks — do not chase the post–Phase 3 spike blind.**

The story has shifted from “COVID cash cow in decline” to “first positive Phase 3 individualized neoantigen (INT) mRNA cancer therapy” after Merck/Moderna’s **19 Aug 2026** INTerpath-001 topline (adjuvant melanoma: RFS + DMFS met vs KEYTRUDA alone). That is a genuine platform re-rate — and the tape has already paid for a large piece of it (~8× off the 52-week lows).

Fundamentals underneath are still a **lossy commercial vaccine business** plus heavy R&D: Q2’26 revenue only **$145M**, net loss **$(782)M**, cash+investments **$6.9B** at 30 Jun 2026 (before the **$950M** July litigation settlement). YE’26 cash guided **$4.7–5.2B**. FY’25 revenue was ~**$1.94B** with a large loss; peak COVID FY’22 was ~**$18.9B**.

**Investment stance:** Core biotech watch name for Luke’s book. Size as a **catalyst / platform** position, not a compounder yet. Prefer adds on drawdowns toward prior consolidation or if INT regulatory path clarifies with less froth. Avoid averaging up into euphoria without a written catalyst map.
