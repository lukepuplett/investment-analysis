## Numbers ($m unless stated, Q3-26 vs Q3-25)
- Revenue 127,577 vs 102,346 (+25%; +25% cc). Headline post's "est $127.93B" not verified here.
- Google Services 96,302 (+11%); Search & other 63,350 (+12%); YouTube ads 11,207 (+9%); Network 7,455 (+1%); subs/platforms/devices 14,290 (+11%).
- **Google Cloud 30,709 vs 15,157 (+103%)**; Cloud operating income 11,121 vs 3,594 (~36% margin). TPU systems sales cited as a meaningful contributor.
- Operating income 40,576 (+30%), margin 31.8%. Other Bets OI (2,047); Alphabet-level (shared AI R&D) (7,655) vs (4,467).
- Other income (expense) **(8,221)** vs +12,759 a year ago, so net income 26,170 (−25%) and **EPS $2.12 vs $2.87**. The diary headline "$2.12 est $2.87 Y/Y" is the prior-year EPS, not a consensus estimate. The drop is below the operating line (non-operating swing of ~$21bn), not operations.
- Cash flow: OCF 42,290 (vs 48,414); capex **51,355** (vs 23,953). **Quarterly FCF ≈ −9.1bn.** 9M capex 131,953 vs 63,596.
- TAC 16,366; headcount 204,757 (+7.7%).
- KPIs: Gemini app 1bn users; first-party APIs ~32bn tokens/min (>4× y/y); 150m YouTube Music/Premium subs incl. trials; "Search query growth highest in five years".

## Call highlights (verbatim)
- **Search slowdown / AI cannibalisation.** Philipp Schindler: "in Q3, Search grew 12% year-over-year to $63 billion... FX. It was the single largest driver of Search deceleration relative to Q2." / "the strong growth in AI Mode queries had an impact because of the lower monetization we were seeing in AI Mode." (The 15% estimate was not mentioned on the call.)
- Sundar Pichai: "the majority of AI Mode queries are incremental, creating more monetization opportunities over time." / "we're now scaling high-quality ads within AI Overviews and AI Mode to more users and queries."
- **Gemini.** Sundar Pichai: "The Gemini app is now at 1 billion users and is the fastest-growing product in Google's history." Anat Ashkenazi: "Google One is our fastest-growing subscription products, driven by demand for AI plans."
- **Cloud backlog.** Anat Ashkenazi: "Google Cloud's backlog was $561 billion in the third quarter, with the majority of the backlog related to typical GCP contracts." / "We expect to recognize just over 55% of the total backlog as revenues over the next 24 months."
- **TPU sales.** Thomas Kurian: "TPU system sales were also a meaningful contributor." Anat Ashkenazi: "we expect TPU system sales to continue to scale in Q4, followed by a meaningful revenue ramp in 2027."
- **Capex.** Anat Ashkenazi: "Our full year 2026 CapEx guidance range of $195 billion to $205 billion remain unchanged." / "We continue to expect our CapEx to increase significantly in 2027, and we'll provide further details on our Q4 call."
- **Negative FCF.** Anat Ashkenazi: "We had negative free cash flow of $9.1 billion in the third quarter, driven by our investments in CapEx." / "Free cash flow in the third quarter was also affected by the aforementioned inventory build to support TPU system sales and payment of the EC fine."
- **Other income −$8.2bn.** Anat Ashkenazi: "Other income and expenses was negative $8.2 billion due primarily to unrealized loss from our investment in SpaceX from a decline in its stock price in the third quarter."
