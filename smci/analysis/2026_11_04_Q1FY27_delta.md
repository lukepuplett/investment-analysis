## Working capital (the FCF question)

- Receivables +$3.56B and inventory +$2.48B (to $15.05B) consumed ~$6.0B.
- That was almost entirely funded by **deferred revenue +$3.05B** (customer advance payments; current deferred revenue $4.33B vs $1.58B in June) plus payables/accruals +$0.92B.
- So OCF looks far better than FY26, but it leans on customer prepayments from very large deployments. If those advances unwind as systems ship, OCF could swing negative again. Watch deferred revenue in Q2.
- New line: **$63.7M preferred dividends** paid (the mandatory convertible preferred raised in FY26).

## Trim-trigger check (from the 25 Sep audit, section "Consider trim (conditional)")

- **(a) Customer One ≥ ~30% with no offsetting enterprise disclosure:** **Met on call commentary** (44%). Formal confirmation waits for the 10-Q (due ~mid-Nov). The company frames it as scale, not dependency, and points to enterprise/DCBBS breadth, but gave no numbers that offset it.
- **(b) Sequential GM collapse without mix explanation:** Not met (12.7%, mix explained).
- **(c) Named hyperscaler exit / share loss:** Not met.

**Verdict: stays WATCH, with the concentration leg now clearly worse while the operating story got better.** One of the three conditional trim triggers has fired on management's own words. Whether to trim, hold or add is **Luke's call**; nothing here is an order. A reasonable middle path to consider is sizing: the position now leans on a single buyer at ~44% of sales, so it behaves more like a contract-manufacturing stake in one AI buildout than a diversified server business.
