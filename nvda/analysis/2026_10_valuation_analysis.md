# NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) – Valuation Analysis

**Analysis date:** 2026-10-02  
**Reference price:** ~$228 (9/30/2026)

## Executive Summary

**BLUF: At ~**$228**, NVDA trades at a **moderate multiple on near-term GAAP earnings power** (~**24×** illustrative on 2× 6M GAAP EPS **$4.85**) while the market prices **multi-year AI infrastructure duration**. **Layer 3** risk: being right on the bottleneck but wrong on **how much FCF is already extracted by suppliers** (a16z chart). **HOLD** assumes **~70% FY2028 revenue growth** (mgmt supply-constrained prelim) is still **under-priced**; **TRIM** if growth decelerates **before** multiple compresses.**
