# NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) – Competitive Analysis

**Analysis date:** 2026-10-02

## Executive Summary

**BLUF: NVDA’s moat is **scale + switching costs + full-stack co-design**, not fab ownership. Merchant GPU share faces **custom silicon** (Broadcom/AVGO hyperscaler XPUs, Google TPU, AMD MI, internal programs) and **networking** competition, but NVDA is expanding **TAM** via Vera CPU, Spectrum-X, and “AI factory” systems — raising $/GW capture at the cost of **complexity and customer bargaining power**.**

**Informal weighted moat:** ~**4.3/5** — **wide but contested** at the largest customers.

## Competitive threats

| Competitor / force | Vector | NVDA response |
|--------------------|--------|---------------|
| **AVGO** (+ peers) | Custom AI ASICs at hyperscalers | Networking + full platform; CUDA fungibility story |
| **Google TPU / others** | Internal accelerators | Open + closed model support; multi-cloud |
| **AMD** | Merchant GPU | Performance/cadence race |
| **Intel / startups** | Inference LPUs (Groq deal noted call week) | Acquire/partner; integrate into stack |
| **TSMC capacity** | Allocation | Long-term supply payments |
