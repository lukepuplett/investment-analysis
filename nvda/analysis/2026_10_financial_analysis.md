# NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) – Financial Analysis

**Analysis date:** 2026-10-02  
**MRQ:** Q2 FY2027 (ended 7/26/2026)

## Executive Summary

**BLUF: Financial quality remains exceptional — **$96.2B** Q2 revenue (+106% YoY), **75%** GAAP gross margin, **$21.3B** quarterly FCF — but working-capital and commitment intensity rose (inventory **~$31.6B**, supply commitments **~$279B**, DSO **60 days**). The bull case is sustained **FCF >> capex**; the bear case is **prepaid supply** meeting a **capex-cliff** if hyperscaler forward FCF stays impaired (see a16z/BofA chart in `diary/2026-09.md`).**
