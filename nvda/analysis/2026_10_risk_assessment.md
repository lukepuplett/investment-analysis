# NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) – Risk Assessment

**Analysis date:** 2026-10-02

## Executive Summary

**BLUF: Top risks are **(1) hyperscaler capex / forward FCF sustainability**, **(2) customer concentration + ASIC substitution**, **(3) China/export controls**, **(4) supply chain (TSMC CoWoS, HBM, memory inflation)**. Any one alone is manageable; **two or more coinciding** would force a **TRIM** review under Luke’s framework.**
