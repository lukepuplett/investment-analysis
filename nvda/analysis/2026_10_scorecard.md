## Key risks (scorecard view)

1. **Hyperscaler concentration & capex sustainability** — top customers drive majority of DC revenue; forward FCF collapsed while semi FCF surged (BofA/a16z, 8/30/2026 data in diary).  
2. **Export controls / China** — **zero** Data Center compute from China in Q3 outlook (8/26/2026).  
3. **Custom ASIC competition** — AVGO, GOOG TPU, in-house programs; NVDA counters with full-stack + networking.  
4. **Supply chain** — TSMC **CoWoS**, **HBM**, memory inflation (Q4 GM trough **71–72%** guided on call).  
5. **Timing** — Market may treat peak earnings as permanent; **stale bottleneck** risk if power/space becomes binding constraint.

## Open questions for the **November ~2026** print (Q3 FY2027)

1. **Revenue vs guide:** Delivery vs **$108.0B ±2%** and path to **~70% FY2028** prelim (supply-constrained).  
2. **Gross margin bridge:** Memory cost impact; progress toward **71–72%** Q4 trough and recovery timing.  
3. **China:** Any change to **DC compute from China** assumption or licensing commentary.  
4. **Customer mix:** Hyperscale **$49B** vs ACIE **$40B** — is diversification real or rotational?  
5. **Vera Rubin:** **~20%** of DC revenue in Q3 (call) — ramp yields and supply.  
6. **Cash conversion:** FCF vs NI; **DSO** and prepayment trends vs **$279B** supply commitments.  
7. **Capex signals:** Do top customers **reiterate** 2026–27 capex on their prints **before/after** NVDA?  
8. **ASIC / competitive:** Management tone on **custom silicon** share and networking attach.
