## BLUF

**HOLD core AI-compute sleeve — add on guide-preserving drawdowns; trim if November print breaks growth/margin trajectory or capex sustainability thesis.**

NVIDIA remains the dominant **AI compute bottleneck** (GPUs, networking, CPUs, full-stack “AI factory”) with record Q2 FY2027 results: **$96.2B** revenue (+106% YoY), **75.0%** GAAP gross margin, **$21.3B** quarterly free cash flow, and Q3 guide **$108.0B ±2%** with **no Data Center compute revenue from China** assumed. Management frames **~70% FY2028 revenue growth** (supply-constrained) and supply tight through **FY2028**.

**Layer 2–3 tension:** Luke’s **a16z/BofA chart (9/30/2026)** shows **hyperscaler 12M forward FCF** collapsing toward **~−$25B** while **semiconductor aggregate FCF** surged to **~$425B** — scrutinise **capex sustainability** even when NVDA beats. Customer **ASIC** programs (AVGO, GOOG, etc.) and **export controls** are the main capture leaks.

**Stance:** **HOLD**; see [`analysis/2026_10_scorecard.md`](analysis/2026_10_scorecard.md) for ADD/TRIM rules and November open questions.
