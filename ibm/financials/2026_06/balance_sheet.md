## Key Notes
- **Goodwill jumped $6.9B (67.7B → 74.6B) and intangible assets rose $2.6B** in six months — driven by the $10.48B "Acquisition of businesses" cash outflow seen in the cash flow statement. This is a meaningfully sized acquisition (or set of acquisitions) for IBM's scale; the next analysis refresh should identify what was acquired and its strategic rationale (likely hybrid-cloud/AI/software tuck-ins consistent with IBM's stated M&A strategy, but not confirmed from the financial statements alone).
- **Cash fell sharply from $13.6B to $7.2B** — largely funding the acquisition spend plus continued dividends ($3.17B paid in H1) and debt paydown, partially offset by new debt issuance.
- Long-term debt rose modestly ($54.8B → $56.2B) — leverage is increasing slightly to help fund the M&A activity, though total equity also grew (+$1.8B) on retained earnings.
- Deferred income (a large recurring liability for IBM, reflecting its subscription/services backlog) held essentially flat ($16.1B → $16.2B current portion) — no material change in forward revenue visibility signal here.
