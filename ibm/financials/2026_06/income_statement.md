## Key Notes
- **This is a steady, unspectacular quarter** — modest revenue growth (+1.1% Q2), flat-to-slightly-down gross margin and net income, in contrast to the sharp moves seen at GLW/HOOD/INTC this cycle. No major surprises in the income statement itself.
- **Sales (products/software) segment revenue declined slightly** (-0.9% YoY) — worth checking whether this reflects a specific product line softness (e.g., mainframe cycle timing, which tends to be lumpy for IBM) before treating it as a trend.
- R&D spend up 10.2% YoY — worth understanding if this is AI/hybrid-cloud related investment ramp.
- Tax provision fell 22.5% YoY on a smaller pre-tax income decline — effective tax rate dropped from ~15.6% to ~12.6%, providing a modest EPS cushion against the revenue softness.
