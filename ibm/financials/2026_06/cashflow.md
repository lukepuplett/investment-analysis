## Key Notes
- **Free cash flow of $7.3B in H1 2026 (+30% YoY)** is a genuinely strong signal — operating cash flow growth (+28% YoY) is outpacing net income growth (+4.1% YoY), consistent with the amortization/deferred-revenue dynamics typical of a subscription-heavy software business.
- **$10.48B spent on business acquisitions in H1 2026** (vs. $7.85B in H1 2025) is the dominant driver of the large cash drawdown this half — IBM continues an active, sizable M&A pace. This is the single most important item to follow up on for the next full analysis refresh (what was acquired, and does it fit the stated hybrid-cloud/AI strategy).
- Dividend remains steady and modestly growing ($3.17B H1 2026 vs $3.11B H1 2025) — no signal of dividend policy change.
- No share buybacks beyond tax-withholding-related repurchases — IBM is prioritizing M&A and the dividend over open-market buybacks this half.
