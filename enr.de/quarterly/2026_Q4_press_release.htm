Earnings Release Q4 FY 2026: Siemens Energy reports a record year and targets a margin of 20 to 22 percent by 2030

“The Siemens Energy team delivered on all raised guidance targets, significantly increased earnings and achieved break-even in our wind business. This provides the foundation for the next phase of our transformation. Our 2030 targets set a clear direction and underscore our successful execution in a favorable market environment. With a dividend of €2.30 per share and through our continuous share buyback program, our shareholders participate in the Company's success,” says Christian Bruch, President and CEO of Siemens Energy AG.

Q4 Fiscal Year 2026

  • Siemens Energy continued its outstanding operational performance and closed fiscal year 2026 with a strong fourth quarter. Orders exceeded the prior-year quarter, revenue reached a new record high, and Profit before Special items more than tripled year-over-year.
  • Orders increased by 5.2% year-over-year on a comparable basis (excluding currency translation and portfolio effects) to €14.8bn. As expected, order intake moderated from the exceptionally strong levels of the first three quarters, reflecting normal project phasing and the large-project nature of the business. Book-to-bill ratio (ratio of orders to revenue) remained clearly above 1. As a result, order backlog increased to a new record level of €164bn at fiscal year-end.
  • • With the highest quarterly revenue ever, totaling €12.8bn, Siemens Energy exceeded the prior-year level by 21.5% on a comparable basis. Both Gas Services and Grid Technologies also achieved all-time high revenue levels.
  • Siemens Energy's Profit before Special items amounted to €1,540m (Q4 FY 2025: €471m). Siemens Gamesa achieved a positive quarterly result for the second consecutive quarter. Special items amounted to positive €184m (Q4 FY 2025: negative €143m), mainly reflecting the reversal of impairments on aeroderivative gas turbine assets. Profit was €1,725m (Q4 FY 2025: €328m).
  • Net income for the quarter totaled €1,360m (Q4 FY 2025: €236m). Corresponding basic earnings per share (EPS) were €1.49 (Q4 FY 2025: €0.19).
  • Free cash flow pre tax increased substantially to €1,636m (Q4 FY 2025: €1,327m). The development was mainly driven by the improved profit. Also, the recent quarter continued to benefit from customer advance payments and reservation fees on the back of continued high order intake.

Fiscal Year 2026

  • Siemens Energy looks back on a record fiscal year 2026. All raised guidance targets were met or exceeded. The business performance of Gas Services and Grid Technologies was exceptional in every respect. Transformation of Industry also surpassed the prior-year levels in terms of volume, profit and cash flow. Siemens Gamesa reached break-even on Profit before Special items as targeted.
  • At €68.1bn, orders were 18.8% above the prior year on a comparable basis. Revenue advanced by 15.5%, likewise on a comparable basis, to €44.2bn, with every segment contributing to growth. Book-to-bill ratio was strong at 1.54 for the fiscal year.
  • Profit before Special items more than doubled to €5,486m (FY 2025: €2,355m). All segments contributed to the improvement, with Siemens Gamesa delivering the strongest progress by achieving break-even. Special items amounted to negative €82m (FY 2025: positive €6m). Profit totaled €5,405m (FY 2025: €2,361m).
  • Net income increased sharply to €4,129m (FY 2025: €1,685m). Corresponding basic EPS were €4.44 (FY 2025: €1.63).
  • Free cash flow pre tax climbed sharply to €8,799m (FY 2025: €4,663m), with all segments improving, led by Gas Services and Grid Technologies.
  • Reflecting the very successful business development, Siemens Energy intends to let its shareholders participate in the Company's success. In addition to the share buyback during the recent fiscal year with a total value of €3.1bn, the Company proposes a dividend of €2.30 per share for fiscal year 2026 (FY 2025: €0.70 per share). This corresponds to around 50% of the Group’s net income attributable to shareholders of Siemens Energy AG, adjusted for extraordinary non-cash effects. In addition, Siemens Energy is expanding its share buyback program and plans to repurchase additionally up to €9bn of its own shares through the end of fiscal year 2030.

Fiscal Year 2027 and mid-term targets Fiscal Year 2030

  • For fiscal year 2027, Siemens Energy expects comparable revenue growth of 14% to 16% and a Profit margin before Special items of 14% to 16%. In addition, the Company anticipates Net income of around €5bn and Free cash flow pre tax of around €10bn.
  • Driven by continued favorable market conditions as well as structural and operational improvements, Siemens Energy has set mid-term growth and profitability targets for fiscal year 2030. The Company aims to achieve a compound annual revenue growth on a comparable basis of 12% to 14% through fiscal year 2030 (i. e., fiscal years 2026 to 2030). For fiscal year 2030, Siemens Energy targets a Profit margin before Special items in the range of 20% to 22%. 

Outlook

For fiscal year 2027, Siemens Energy expects the favorable trends in the energy sector to continue. Global electricity demand is set to rise further, driven by the electrification of transport, heating, cooling and industry, as well as by new large consumers such as data centers. The build-out of data centers to support the adoption of artificial intelligence is likely to lead to further investment in power generation capacity and grid infrastructure, especially in North America. At the same time, the need for security of energy supply continues to support investment in reliable generation and resilient grids. The gas turbine market is expected to remain robust, supported by data centers, government programs for grid stability and coal phase-out, as well as industrial growth. Investment in grid infrastructure is expected to remain at high levels, driven by electrification, the expansion of renewable energies and the modernization of aging grids. Siemens Energy therefore expects Gas Services and Grid Technologies in particular to benefit from these developments.

For fiscal year 2027, for Siemens Energy a comparable revenue growth (excluding currency translation and portfolio effects) in the range of 14% to 16% (actual figure FY 2026: 15.5%) and a Profit margin before Special items between 14% and 16% (actual figure FY 2026: 12.4%) are forecasted. In addition, a Net income of around €5bn (actual figure FY 2026: €4,129m) and a Free cash flow pre tax of around €10bn (actual figure FY 2026: €8,799m) are expected.

The outlook for Siemens Energy does not include charges related to any future legal and regulatory matters.

Assumptions per business area

  • Gas Services assumes a comparable revenue growth of 24% to 26% (actual figure FY 2026: 20.3%) and a Profit margin before Special items of 18% to 20% (actual figure FY 2026: 16.4%).
  • Grid Technologies plans to achieve a comparable revenue growth of 22% to 24% (actual figure FY 2026: 26.2%) and a Profit margin before Special items between 21% and 23% (actual figure FY 2026: 18.9%).
  • Transformation of Industry expects a comparable revenue growth of 0% to 2% (actual figure FY 2026: 3.4%) and a Profit margin before Special items of 11% to 13% (actual figure FY 2026: 12.1%).
  • Siemens Gamesa assumes a comparable revenue growth of negative 3% to negative 1% (actual figure FY 2026: positive 6.7%) and a Profit margin before Special items of 0% to 2% (actual figure FY 2026: 0.1%).
