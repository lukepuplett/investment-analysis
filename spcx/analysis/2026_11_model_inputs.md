## Executive notes

### Baker orbital-compute arithmetic (TBPN, 15 Jun 2026)

Gavin Baker stated terrestrial AI build cost of **$60B/GW**, split **$35B IT** + **$25B power/cooling** (no separate land/shell), and orbit case **$5B/GW launch** once Starship is reusable, implying **~$30B/GW** orbit ([TBPN interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PW5n3ZnEJN0); see also [diary Baker entry](../diary/2026-11.md) 2026-11-11).

**Arithmetic check:** From Baker’s own splits, orbit all-in is **$35B IT + $5B launch = $40B/GW**, not $30B. The $30B figure only works if $25B of terrestrial power/cooling is fully avoided **and** launch is $5B **and** IT stays $35B while something else drops by $10B—which he does not specify. Consistent alternative: **$60B − $25B (avoided power/cooling) + $5B launch = $40B/GW** orbit vs $60B terrestrial (~1.5× cheaper, not 2×).

Google **$50B/GW** “monetization” cited on the podcast is **not** in the Google Cloud FWP; the signed run-rate there is **$920M/month** for ~110k GPUs (see register). Baker’s $50B/GW was described as an Altimeter-style **terrestrial** hosting comparison, not a filed contract rate.

## Biggest data gaps (for modeling)

1. **Customer identity mapping** — 10-Q only labels Customer A/B; Google and Anthropic are strongly indicated by FWPs and Anthropic S-1 but not named in SpaceX quarterly filings.
2. **GPU / MW per customer contract** — Google FWP gives GPU count; Anthropic S-1 gives dollars, not MW or GPU count at full run-rate.
3. **$40B Apollo lease economics** — 8-K states aggregate principal; interest rate, lessor return, and mapping to `Other financings` $12,979M on balance sheet need the definitive lease docs or a subsequent 10-Q footnote.
4. **Orbital compute capex and revenue** — Strategy and Musk award milestones reference 100 TW/year non-Earth compute; no capex budget or pricing model in filings.
5. **Launch cadence & $/kg realized** — Prospectus gives vehicle specs; no filed schedule for Starship commercial reuse cadence or audited $/kg.
6. **Q1 FY2026 standalone 10-Q** — Not filed (pre-IPO); YTD financials begin effectively at IPO/Q2 with heavy pro forma noise from Cursor.
7. **Musk public “$100B ARR December” and “$500B–$1T exit 2027”** — **NOT FOUND** in downloaded primary sources; required for bull-case revenue models if taken literally.
8. **Starlink enterprise/mobile unique devices** — Press release table partially captured; full enterprise/mobile KPIs need clean extract from Exhibit 99.1 tables.
