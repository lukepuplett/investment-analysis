# TSLA–SpaceX merger watch: special committee terms — 25 Nov 2026

**Trigger:** any 8-K or proxy mentioning the committees; monthly check through Q1 2027.

## Status
- **FT, 24 Nov (via Reuters / Investing.com):** the companies have named five directors to two special committees: Tesla — Robyn Denholm, Kathleen Wilson-Thompson, Jack Hartung; SpaceX — Roelof Botha, Randy Glein. Advisers: Morgan Stanley and Goldman Sachs for SpaceX, Evercore for Tesla. All-stock deal, about $3.5T combined, reportedly aiming at a year-end timetable, but the committees are "not close" to agreement. Reuters could not verify the FT report. Sources: https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/spacex-tesla-directors-negotiate-potential-35-trillion-merger-ft-reports-5047091 ; https://thenextweb.com/news/spacex-tesla-merger-five-directors-special-committees
- **Musk** barred from formally negotiating (Texas rules); SpaceX said in October the deal also needs his approval as controlling holder (about 84% of SpaceX votes vs about 20% of Tesla's).
- **Majority-of-minority:** none reported. The reported Tesla vote needs more than 50% of all possible votes (abstentions count against), at a separate extraordinary meeting rather than the 3 Dec annual meeting. No public condition requiring a majority of unaffiliated holders has been seen. **No 8-K or proxy has been filed that I could find.**
- **Exchange ratio:** none agreed. Market-implied on 24 Nov: **3.0** SpaceX per Tesla share (TSLA $386.83, SPCX $128.84; Yahoo Finance article). On 25 Nov Yahoo chart closes: TSLA $384.68, SPCX $125.81 = **3.06**. The 11 Nov diary had 2.84.
- Commentary only, not terms: HSBC math about $507 per TSLA (about 3.9 at current prices); one retail bull wants 4.2 and has since called it unrealistic. A proposal is expected after SpaceX's last IPO lock-up ends **8 Dec** (commentary, not confirmed).
