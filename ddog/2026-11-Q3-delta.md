## Guidance (as of 4 Nov 2026)
- **Q4 2026:** revenue **$1.19–1.20bn** (~25–26% y/y; ~flat to slightly down sequential vs Q3); non-GAAP OI **$280–290m**; non-GAAP EPS **$0.69–0.71** (~379m diluted shares).
- **FY 2026:** revenue **$4.515–4.525bn** (~32% y/y; raised vs August); non-GAAP OI **$1.095–1.105bn**; non-GAAP EPS **$2.73–2.75** (~375m diluted shares). FY midpoints above Street (~$4.48bn / ~$2.53).

## Tape / why it sold off
- Closed **$188.72 (−14.4%)** on 4 Nov (Yahoo), volume ~13.2m, after a multi-day slide into the print. Classic beat-and-guide-down: Q4 midpoint implies near-zero sequential growth.
- Call colour (secondary): guide assumes the **largest (unnamed) AI-native customer** spends only at its **contractual minimum** in Q4; usage fell in Q3 but stayed above the floor. Early renewals / pricing trade-offs at some large AI accounts also near-term headwinds. Ex that customer, implied Q4 growth said to be low- to mid-30s. Press also cited call figures of **RPO ~$3.85bn (+38%)**, billings ~$1.16bn (+30%), Infra Monitoring suite **>$2bn ARR**, and **>850 AI customers** — confirm in 10-Q / transcript.

## Delta vs thesis (`analysis/2025_12_investment_thesis.md`, HOLD/accumulate; May 2026 vintage)
- Growth pillar **intact and stronger than the May 2026 thesis’s ~20%+ runway framing**: Q3 +35%, FY guide ~32%. AI-observability pillar gets a product proof-point (Scout/Bits) and continued large-customer count growth.
- Near-term risk is **customer concentration / consumption volatility** at one AI-native whale — same shape of risk that hit the August print. Q4 guide is deliberately conservative on that account; stock re-rated hard (−14% day-of, and already down sharply into the print).
- Profitability ahead of the old “FY2027 inflection” story: non-GAAP OM already 28% in Q3, FY non-GAAP OI guided >$1.0bn.
- **Thesis not invalidated; multiple compressed on guide/concentration.** Hold/add/trim is Luke’s call. Key watch: does that largest customer renew/expand, and does ex-whale growth stay mid/high-20s+.
