================================================================================
SEGMENT PERFORMANCE (FY2025)
================================================================================

CIVIL AEROSPACE
- Revenue: £12.7B (~60% of group)
- Operating margin improved significantly
- Large engine EFH at 115-120% of 2019 levels
- Shop visits: 1,440 (+10% YoY)
- Pearl business aviation: 224 engines delivered (+26% YoY)
- LTSA margin expansion on track (19pp contract margin increase 2022-2028)

DEFENCE
- Revenue: £2.1B (~10% of group)
- Order backlog: £17.4B (3+ years visibility)
- Book-to-bill: 1.1x
- Eurofighter EJ200 sustainment performing well
- Submarine programs on schedule

POWER SYSTEMS
- Revenue: £4.9B (~15% of group; fastest growing, +19% YoY)
- Power generation revenue: +30% YoY
- Data center demand driving growth
- Battery storage (BESS) achieved breakeven
- Series 4000 higher power density engine on track for 2028

================================================================================
SHAREHOLDER RETURNS
================================================================================

- Dividend: 9.5p per share (first meaningful dividend post-turnaround)
- Share buyback: £1bn completed in 2025
- NEW: £7–9bn share buyback program announced for 2026–2028
  - £2.5bn tranche allocated for 2026
  - First multi-year buyback in Rolls-Royce history
- Target: 75% total shareholder return (dividend + buyback) by 2028
