**Period:** Half-year ended 30 June 2026  
**Released:** 26 August 2026  
**Source:** https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20260826/pdf/0737mzsvyrtjcn.pdf

---

## Executive Summary

**BLUF: DroneShield reported record 1H revenue of A$125.8M (+74% YoY) but swung to a A$32.2M statutory loss (vs A$2.1M profit 1H25) due to A$8.7M disruption costs from leadership transition and ASIC investigation. Gross margin compressed to 60% (from 65.3%). Cash position declined to A$180M. FY2026 guidance of A$250–270M reaffirmed with A$240M committed. New CEO Angus Bean focused on stabilization and margin recovery.**
