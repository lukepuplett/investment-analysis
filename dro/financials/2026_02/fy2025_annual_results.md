## Executive Summary

**BLUF: DroneShield delivered record FY2025 results with A$216.5M revenue (+276% YoY), turning profitable with A$3.5M statutory NPAT and A$36.5M underlying EBITDA. ## Segment Analysis

- **Europe:** ~45% (largest driver, 66+ projects in pipeline)

- **Americas:** ~20% (U.S. DoD, homeland security)
- **Middle East/Other:** ~10%

### Revenue by Product Type
- **Hardware (DroneGun, DroneSentry-X, RfPatrol):** ~80%
- **Software/SaaS (DroneSentry-C2, subscriptions):** ~7%
- **Third-party Integration (radars, cameras):** ~13%
