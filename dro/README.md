# DroneShield (ASX:DRO) - Investment Analysis

## Company Overview
DroneShield Limited (ABN 608 915 859) is an Australian company specializing in drone detection and counter-drone technology, operating as the only publicly listed pure-play counter-drone company globally.

**Primary Listing:** ASX:DRO  
**OTC:** DRSHF  
**IR:** https://www.droneshield.com/investor-relations

## Executive Summary (BLUF - Bottom Line Up Front)

### Current Assessment (September 2026)

**BLUF: DroneShield delivered record 1H 2026 revenue of A$125.8M (+74% YoY) but swung to a A$32.2M statutory loss due to A$8.7M one-off disruption costs (leadership transition, ASIC investigation). Gross margin compressed to 60% (vs 65% prior). Share price has declined ~65% from Jan 2025 peaks. Founding CEO departed April 2026; ASIC investigation ongoing. FY2026 guidance of A$250–270M reaffirmed with A$240M committed. The company retains technology leadership and market position, but governance concerns and profitability reversal have damaged investor confidence. HOLD – pending resolution of regulatory matters and margin recovery evidence.**
