## Profitability Analysis

### Statutory Loss Breakdown
The A$32.2M statutory net loss includes:
- **A$8.7M disruption costs** – one-off costs related to leadership transition, governance review, and operational disruptions
- **Underlying business** remained operationally sound but margins compressed

### Gross Margin Compression
- 1H 2026: 60.0% vs 1H 2025: 65.3%
- Driven by: product mix shift, third-party integration components, scaling costs
- Management expects margin stabilization as product mix normalizes
