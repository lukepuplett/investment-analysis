**Analysis Date:** September 2026  
**Prior Reference:** Q3 2025 / January 2025 analysis  
**Current Data:** 1H FY2026 (30 June 2026)

---

## Executive Summary

**BLUF: Significant deterioration in investment thesis since Jan 2025 "STRONG BUY" rating. While revenue growth continues (+74% 1H YoY), the company has swung to statutory losses (A$32.2M 1H26), gross margins compressed 530 bps, ASIC investigation is ongoing, founding CEO departed, and share price has declined ~65% from Jan 2025 levels (~A$4.68 → A$1.66). Thesis pillars around profitability and execution are now challenged. Recommend downgrade to HOLD pending resolution of regulatory matters and margin recovery evidence.**

## Updated Recommendation

### Prior Rating (Jan 2025): **STRONG BUY**
- Target: A$30 (540% upside from A$4.68)
- Thesis: Market leadership, explosive growth, TAM opportunity

### Current Assessment (Sep 2026): **HOLD / NEUTRAL**

**Rationale:**
1. **Governance overhang** – ASIC investigation outcome unknown
2. **Leadership transition** – new CEO unproven at scale
3. **Margin compression** – 60% gross margin vs 65%+ expectation
4. **Profitability reversal** – statutory losses despite revenue growth
5. **Valuation reset** – 65% decline has de-risked valuation but growth deceleration offsets

**What Would Upgrade to BUY:**
- ASIC investigation resolved with no material penalties
- 2H 2026 gross margin recovery to 62%+
- Return to underlying EBITDA positive
- Evidence new CEO executing effectively

**What Would Downgrade to SELL:**
- ASIC penalties >A$20M or material remediation required
- Further margin compression to <58%
- Cash burn accelerates (cash <A$150M)
- Loss of major contracts or market share
