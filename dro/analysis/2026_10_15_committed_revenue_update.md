## Operational points

- **RfRecon:** first deliveries (Tier 1 Western European military end-user) still on track for this quarter / FY26.
- **RfAI-3:** software on track for delivery by end-2026.
- **Mission Ready Services** launched 1 Oct 2026. It is a subscription (updates, training, support, portal) covering all solutions except DroneGuns. There were 4,200 software-enabled devices at 12 Oct (4,100 at 1H).
- **People:** headcount 519 at 30 Sep (537 at 30 Jun). New CFO Rebecca Lowde starts 2 Nov.
- **CEO (Angus Bean):** "This is an encouraging update as we approach the end of the year". He also pointed to the A$29M / A$17M forward committed book.

## Still open (unchanged by this update)

- **Margin:** 1H26 GM 60.0% (65.3% 1H25), hit by third-party product mix and ERP/relocation inventory impairment. Management lists levers (next-gen pricing, Mission Ready Services, ERP inventory control) but has given no new margin number.
- **Profitability:** 1H26 underlying EBITDA was a loss of A$12.4M (profit of A$8.0M in 1H25).
- **Cash:** A$180M, no debt at 30 Jun. The Q3 4C will show receipts and burn for Jul–Sep.
- **ASIC investigation:** no update in this pack.

## What the Q3 4C needs to show

1. Customer receipts that line up with the committed book (cash conversion, not just orders).
2. Operating cash burn small enough to keep cash well above ~A$150M.
3. Any margin or cost commentary (opex base after the headcount trim).
