## What changed since the Sep 2026 delta (`analysis/2026_09_delta_analysis.md`)
- **CEO change is now permanent.** Wayne Tupuola went on a three-month health leave on 16 Jun 2026, with Ann Tewari as Interim President (8-K 23 Jun). He then **resigned as CEO for medical reasons, effective 13 Oct 2026**. The Sep docs and README said he "departed" in June. That was a leave, and it is corrected here. Source: 8-K filed 16 Oct 2026, accession 0001493152-26-047427, Ex 99.1.
- **Founder Dmitri Nikitin returns as Executive Chairman and principal executive officer** (appointed 12 Oct). There will be no separate CEO. His pay is a $400k base plus a **2,000,000-share award**: 50% vests at grant and the rest over two anniversaries, with accelerated vesting on a no-cause exit. His stated priorities are LSAD (Laser Shield Anti-Drone), engineering, building a sales organisation, and integrating the acquisitions (CMS and others) into one organisation. Tewari goes back to VP Operations & Strategy. Same 8-K.
- **CFO made permanent.** Timothy Peterman was Acting CFO from 8 Sep and became permanent effective 6 Oct, on a $300k base and a 3-year agreement. The company also hired Sheppard Mullin as securities counsel and CBIZ for financial-reporting and internal-control consulting. Source: 8-K filed 20 Oct 2026, accession 0001493152-26-047691. Context: this is the fourth principal financial officer in 2026 (CFO Portal/Roman Franklin from May, terminated 24 Jun; Ralph Venegas; then Peterman). Peterman was CEO of iMedia Brands, which filed Chapter 11 in June 2023 (disclosed in the 8 Sep 8-K).
- **New Nasdaq deficiency: minimum bid price.** On 20 Oct Nasdaq notified the company that the bid had been below $1.00 for 30 consecutive business days. It has until **19 Apr 2027** to regain compliance (10+ consecutive days at $1.00 or more), possibly with a second 180-day period after that. There is no immediate effect on trading. A reverse split is the usual cure and would be another dilution/sentiment event. Source: 8-K filed 23 Oct 2026, accession 0001493152-26-048238.
