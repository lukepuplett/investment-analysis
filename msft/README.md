# Microsoft Corporation (MSFT)

## Company Overview
Microsoft Corporation is a technology company that develops and supports software, services, devices, and solutions worldwide. The company operates through three segments: Productivity and Business Processes (Office, LinkedIn, Dynamics), Intelligent Cloud (Azure, server products, enterprise services), and More Personal Computing (Windows, devices, gaming, search advertising). **Note:** Effective FY2027, segments reorganize into Agents & Infra and Devices & Consumer (per 8-K dated 2 Sep 2026).

**Ticker:** MSFT  
**Exchange:** NASDAQ  
**Sector:** Technology  
**Industry:** Software—Infrastructure / Cloud / Productivity  

## Executive Summary (BLUF - Bottom Line Up Front)

**Updated BLUF (September 2026):** Microsoft delivered exceptional FY2026 results with revenue of **$331.8B (+18%)**, Azure exceeding **$100B annual revenue (+41%)**, and AI ARR reaching **$37B+** with M365 Copilot exceeding **30 million seats**. RPO (backlog) surged to **$678B (+84% YoY)**, indicating unprecedented forward visibility. Q4 FY26 Azure growth accelerated to **43%**, defying law of large numbers expectations. Stock at **~$495** trades at **~27.6x trailing P/E / ~25x forward** — premium valuation justified by growth acceleration and AI leadership. CapEx guidance of **~$175B for CY2026** reflects massive AI infrastructure investment, compressing TTM FCF to ~$67B (down from $84.5B). Balance sheet remains fortress (~$90B cash/ST investments). **HOLD/BUY** for long-term investors; Base Case fair value ~$500–520/share; key risks are CapEx execution and regulatory scrutiny.
