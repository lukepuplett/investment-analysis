# MSFT — FY27 Q1 (quarter to 30 Sep 2026) post-print delta (28 Oct 2026)

Source: Microsoft 8-K Ex 99.1 filed 28 Oct 2026 ([EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526436122/msft-ex99_1.htm)); 10-Q also filed 28 Oct, not yet reconciled. Light delta note only.

## Numbers
- Revenue $92.3bn (+19%); operating income $45.9bn (+21%, +22% cc). Matches the diary headline post (vs est $90.63bn / $43.92bn, estimates unverified).
- Net income GAAP $36.6bn (+32%), non-GAAP ex-OpenAI $36.4bn (+18%). EPS GAAP $4.92, non-GAAP $4.89 (+18%). OpenAI impact: +$207m gain this quarter vs a $3.1bn loss a year ago.
- New segment reporting: **Agents and Infra $76.8bn (+25%)**; Devices and Consumer $15.5bn (−3%).
- **Azure +48%**; Microsoft Cloud $63.9bn (+30%, accelerating); M365 commercial cloud +18% adj (+17% reported); M365 consumer cloud +17%; Windows OEM & Devices −22%; Xbox content −2%; search ex-TAC +12%.
- **Commercial RPO $677bn (+73%).**
- Cash flow: OCF $58.0bn (vs $45.1bn); additions to PP&E $39.4bn (vs $19.4bn), so FCF ≈ $18.6bn (vs $25.7bn). Finance leases not included in that capex figure.
- Returned $10.2bn via dividends and buybacks.

## Delta vs thesis
- Azure re-accelerating to 48% and a $677bn backlog support the AI-infrastructure thesis; capex doubling is compressing FCF.
- Windows OEM −22% is the weak spot (PC cycle). Guidance was on the call; not captured here.
- Thesis unchanged. Hold/trim is Luke's call.

## Call highlights (verbatim)
- **Azure capacity.** Amy Hood: "Customer demand continues to exceed available supply." / "Azure revenue was ahead of expectations and increased 48%, even with the 3P capacity delays noted earlier, driven by Foundry and improved CPU monetization." / "we had some supply we were counting on Q2 move out into H2."
- **RPO.** Amy Hood: "Commercial remaining performance obligation grew 73% to $677 billion, with a smaller sequential decline relative to historical seasonality due to the large Azure commitment this quarter." / "Our RPO has a weighted average duration of 2.3 years and roughly 30% will be recognized in revenue in the next 12 months, up 29% year-over-year."
- **Capex.** Amy Hood: "Capital expenditures were $45.2 billion as some third-party capacity deliveries we expected in Q1 were delayed." / "In Q2, we expect CapEx spend will be over $50 billion." / "for the calendar year, spend should be approximately $170 billion due to the 3P capacity delays noted earlier."
- **OpenAI.** Satya Nadella: "Azure OpenAI directly benefited in the last quarter with big advances in OpenAI models." Gabriela Borges (analyst): "you have OpenAI at 11% of revenue in the quarter."
- **Windows OEM.** Amy Hood: "Windows OEM and devices revenue decreased 22%." / "Windows OEM revenue was below expectations and decreased 27% and 28% in constant currency, driven by the Windows 10 end of support demand in the prior year, inventory drawdowns by OEM and channel partners and weaker PC market demand." / Guide: "Windows OEM and devices revenue should decline in the mid-30s."
