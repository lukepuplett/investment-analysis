# MSFT — FY27 Q1 (quarter to 30 Sep 2026) post-print delta (28 Oct 2026)

Source: Microsoft 8-K Ex 99.1 filed 28 Oct 2026 ([EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526436122/msft-ex99_1.htm)); 10-Q also filed 28 Oct, not yet reconciled. Light delta note only.

## Numbers
- Revenue $92.3bn (+19%); operating income $45.9bn (+21%, +22% cc). Matches the diary headline post (vs est $90.63bn / $43.92bn, estimates unverified).
- Net income GAAP $36.6bn (+32%), non-GAAP ex-OpenAI $36.4bn (+18%). EPS GAAP $4.92, non-GAAP $4.89 (+18%). OpenAI impact: +$207m gain this quarter vs a $3.1bn loss a year ago.
- New segment reporting: **Agents and Infra $76.8bn (+25%)**; Devices and Consumer $15.5bn (−3%).
- **Azure +48%**; Microsoft Cloud $63.9bn (+30%, accelerating); M365 commercial cloud +18% adj (+17% reported); M365 consumer cloud +17%; Windows OEM & Devices −22%; Xbox content −2%; search ex-TAC +12%.
- **Commercial RPO $677bn (+73%).**
- Cash flow: OCF $58.0bn (vs $45.1bn); additions to PP&E $39.4bn (vs $19.4bn), so FCF ≈ $18.6bn (vs $25.7bn). Finance leases not included in that capex figure.
- Returned $10.2bn via dividends and buybacks.

## Delta vs thesis
- Azure re-accelerating to 48% and a $677bn backlog support the AI-infrastructure thesis; capex doubling is compressing FCF.
- Windows OEM −22% is the weak spot (PC cycle). Guidance was on the call; not captured here.
- Thesis unchanged. Hold/trim is Luke's call.
