## Executive Summary

**BLUF: Microsoft delivered exceptional FY2026 results with revenue of $331.8B (+18%), Azure exceeding $100B annual revenue (+41%), and AI ARR reaching $37B+ with M365 Copilot exceeding 30 million seats. RPO (backlog) surged to $678B (+84% YoY), indicating unprecedented forward visibility. The investment thesis remains intact with AI monetization validated. CapEx guidance of ~$175B for CY2026 reflects massive infrastructure investment for AI. Segment reorganization (FY2027) ends historical comparability. Stock at ~$495 trades at ~27.6x trailing P/E, ~25x forward — premium valuation justified by growth acceleration and AI leadership. Maintain HOLD/BUY for long-term investors.**
