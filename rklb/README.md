## Executive Summary (BLUF - Bottom Line Up Front)

**September 16, 2026 Update: ACCUMULATE / BUILD.** Rocket Lab printed record Q2 2026 revenue of **$234.1M** (+62% YoY) and record backlog of **$2.36B**, guided Q3 to **$250–265M**, and is flying Electron at high cadence (95th mission / 16th of 2026 on 11 Sep — 100% YTD success). Material corporate shift: the pending **~$8B cash-and-stock Iridium** acquisition is now **fully financed** (15 Sep — $1.944B ATM ≈29.3M shares; Iridium $1.775B term loan CoC amendment; $3.6B bridge terminated; close targeted mid-2027). That expands the thesis from “neutral launcher + Space Systems” into **launch + spacecraft + satellite services**, at the cost of near-term dilution and integration/regulatory risk. **Neutron** first-stage tank is aimed at LC-3 Wallops delivery in **Q4 2026**, but management says the 2026 first-flight window is **narrowing** (2027 slip increasingly likely) — schedule risk is up, binary risk deferred not removed. Stock has reset from $100 (May) to ~$62 (~52x TTM P/S vs ~95x), now **below** the May Neutral Provider fair-value band ($77.50–93.50). Updated FV framework **$55–85** (base ~$65–75). Conviction **6.5/10** (was 7/10): stronger ops, weaker schedule clarity, Iridium double-edged. Other Sep items: IMM Apex germanium-free solar cells (~31.5% BOL); GAO protest of NASA’s ~$700M Mars Telecommunications Network award to Blue Origin (work paused).

**Rating change vs May 2026 README:** Conditional “ACCUMULATE if Neutral Provider / WAIT $75–85” → **ACCUMULATE / BUILD** at ~$62 (margin of safety improved; dilution and Neutron slip are the taxes).

## Company Overview

Rocket Lab is a vertically integrated end-to-end space company:

- **Launch:** Electron (small-lift), HASTE (hypersonic test), Neutron (medium-lift reusable — in development), GHOST portable system (Kodiak LC-4 planned).
- **Space Systems:** Spacecraft (incl. Flatellite, GEO), payloads, subsystems (solar, propulsion, optics/laser via SolAero/Mynaric, mechanisms via Motiv).
- **Pending:** Iridium Communications — LEO constellation, L-band spectrum, satellite services (close mid-2027 target).
