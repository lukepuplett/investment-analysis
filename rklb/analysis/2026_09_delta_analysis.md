## WHAT CHANGED SINCE MAY 2026

### 1. Q2 Execution Validated the Ops Flywheel ✅
- Beat sequential and held >60% YoY growth.
- Backlog to $2.36B; ~45.5% expected to convert in 12 months (call).
- Space Force SB-AMTI Flatellite+Neutron (~$397M incl. options); GEO SDA contracts >$160M.
- **Thesis impact:** Space Systems + government pillars remain **Strong** (9/10 and 8/10).

### 2. Iridium — Thesis Expansion + Dilution ⚠️✅
- ~$8B cash-and-stock deal announced with Q2 materials; **fully financed 15 Sep 2026**.
- $1.944B ATM (~29.3M shares); Iridium $1.775B term loan CoC amendment; **$3.6B bridge terminated**.
- Close mid-2027 pending approvals; S-4 effective 26 Aug 2026.
- Strategic logic: Iridium LEO + L-band spectrum + subscribers → launch + spacecraft + **services/applications** (company “space powerhouse” framing). Complements Neutral Provider launch thesis with owned constellation economics.
- **Risks:** equity issued into a falling tape; stock consideration at close = further dilution; regulatory (FCC/CFIUS/antitrust) and integration of a mature FCF telecom into a growth aerospace culture; leverage via guaranteed Iridium term loan.
- **Thesis impact:** New pillar **“Services / Constellation Operator”** at **5/10** confidence until close. Dilution is the near-term tax on existing holders.

### 3. Neutron Timeline Risk Increased ⚠️
- May README: Q4 2026 first flight as inflection.
- Aug Q2 PR/call: Stage 1 tank aligned for **pad delivery Q4 2026**; management: 2026 flight window **narrowing**; prioritize reliable cadence over rushing Flight 1.
- Independent coverage (Spaceflight Now 10 Aug 2026): 2027 debut increasingly plausible.
- Archimedes 400+ hot-fires; Hungry Hippo testing complete (per IR/diary).
- **Thesis impact:** Neutron pillar confidence **6/10 → 5/10**. Binary risk deferred, not removed — delay cedes medium-lift window to Relativity / New Glenn.

### 4. Electron Ops — Cadence Confirmed ✅
- 16 missions YTD 2026 through 11 Sep; 95th Electron; 100% success YTD.
- Synspective constellation campaign continuing (“Owl By The Dozen” ~19 Sep).
- **Thesis impact:** Electron moat **8/10** intact; commercial repeatability proven.

### 5. IMM Apex (8 Sep) — Subsystems Moat ✅
- Germanium-free IMM Apex cells, ~31.5% BOL, lower mass, production volumes.
- Supports vertical integration / merchant solar story and constellation power budgets.
- Incremental positive for Space Systems / subsystems; not thesis-changing alone.

### 6. GAO Protest of NASA MTN (11 Sep) ⚠️
- Protest of ~$700M Mars Telecommunications Network award to Blue Origin; NASA work paused.
- Upside if sustained/recompete; base case most GAO protests fail — treat as **option**, not backlog.
- **Thesis impact:** Neutral-to-slightly-negative optics vs Blue Origin; no P&L impact yet.

### 7. Neutral Provider Thesis — Still Live, Less Alone
- SpaceX/xAI conflict thesis from May README not falsified.
- Iridium adds a twist: RKLB becomes a **comms operator**, slightly less “pure Switzerland,” but still not an AI-compute competitor. Monitor whether big-tech customers perceive conflict.
- At ~$62, market is **not** pricing a full Neutral Provider + Iridium synergies bull case.
