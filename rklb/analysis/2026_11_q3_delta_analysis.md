## Backlog and contracts
- Backlog about **$3.57B**, +52% sequentially (+$1.2B). About $1B of new launch contracts (Electron, HASTE, Neutron) and $470M of Space Systems.
- Launch backlog 117 launches. Neutron backlog 16 missions after >$515M of Q3 signings; base price raised to $70M on several missions.
- **Neutron first launch now no earlier than 2027** (slip; the Sep note carried the schedule risk).
- 12 Rocket Lab-built spacecraft launched in the period, including eight for Globalstar. More than 55 spacecraft in build.

## Balance sheet and Iridium
Cash and equivalents **$4.45B** (from $0.83B at year-end 2025) plus about $256M of marketable securities. The ATM equity raise fully funds the pending Iridium acquisition. Iridium lenders approved keeping its credit facility, and Iridium shareholders approved the deal. Dilution is real: Q4 weighted shares guided at 681M including about 41M Series A convertible participating preferred.
