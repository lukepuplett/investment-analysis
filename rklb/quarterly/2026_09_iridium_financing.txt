SUMMARY
================================================================================

Rocket Lab announced it has fully financed the pending ~$8B cash-and-stock acquisition
of Iridium Communications Inc. (announced with merger agreement ~June 28, 2026).

KEY ACTIONS (as of 15 Sep 2026)
1. Completed ATM equity offering: ~$1.944B gross proceeds via ~29.3M shares
   (before commissions/expenses). Proceeds primarily for cash consideration.
2. Iridium amended its existing $1.775B term loan (as of 6/30/26) — Change of Control
   Amendment carves out Rocket Lab's acquisition; Rocket Lab USA to provide unsecured
   parent guarantee at closing; margins step up within stated ranges; exit/prepay fees.
3. Terminated $3.6B senior secured bridge commitment (Deutsche Bank / Wells Fargo
   commitment letter dated June 28, 2026) in full on Sep 15, 2026.

CLOSE TIMING
- Expected mid-2027, subject to regulatory and shareholder approvals.
- S-4 declared effective Aug 26, 2026; Iridium definitive proxy / Rocket Lab prospectus filed.

STRATEGIC THESIS (company framing)
Combine Rocket Lab launch + spacecraft manufacturing with Iridium's global LEO
communications constellation, L-band spectrum, and subscriber base → vertically
integrated launch + spacecraft + satellite services / applications platform.

DILUTION / CAPITAL STRUCTURE NOTES (analyst)
- 29.3M new common shares ≈ ~5% of ~598M common outstanding at 6/30/26 (larger % of
  free float); plus stock consideration to Iridium holders at close (material additional
  dilution not quantified here — see S-4).
- Bridge kill removes near-term secured-debt overhang but Iridium term loan (~$1.8B)
  remains in consolidated structure post-close with RKLB USA guarantee.
- ATM done into a soft tape (~$62 vs May peak ~$100 / $151 52-week high) — expensive
  equity from a May-peak perspective, but de-risks deal certainty vs bridge financing.
