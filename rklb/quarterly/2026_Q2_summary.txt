---

## Strategic Events Embedded in Q2 Narrative

1. **Iridium acquisition announced** (late June / Q2 close period): ~$8B cash-and-stock to combine launch + satellite manufacturing with Iridium LEO constellation, L-band spectrum, and subscribers. Close targeted mid-2027.
2. **Mynaric & Motiv closed** — optical laser terminals (Europe footprint) + robotics/mechanisms.
3. **Neutron:** Stage 1 tank production aligned for pad delivery LC-3 Wallops **Q4 2026**; management said 2026 first-flight window is **narrowing**; priority is service-ready cadence over rushing Flight 1 (slip to 2027 increasingly plausible).
4. **Space Force SB-AMTI** Flatellite + Neutron award (~$397M incl. options).
5. **GEO sats:** >$160M for three GEO spacecraft incl. SSC space-domain-awareness prime.
6. **GHOST** portable launch system; LC-4 Kodiak Alaska; suborbital debut 2027.
