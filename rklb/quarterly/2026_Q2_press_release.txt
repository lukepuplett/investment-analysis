HEADLINE
Rocket Lab Announces Second Quarter 2026 Financial Results: Posts Record Revenue
and Record Backlog, Guides To Another Record Revenue Quarter in Q3 2026
Long Beach, California — August 10, 2026
================================================================================

CEO (Sir Peter Beck) key quotes:
- Record $234M Q2 revenue — up 62% YoY and $34M higher than prior-quarter record.
- Backlog $2.36B — another record; combined with post-close deals = more than $1B
  in new contracts across launch and space systems already entered into in Q3.
- Closed Mynaric and Motiv; announced agreement to acquire Iridium Communications Inc.
  to become a self-launching, tier-1 space power delivering communications to millions.

HIGHLIGHTS (Q2 2026 + post-period updates cited in PR)
- Record quarterly revenue $234M (+62% YoY).
- Record backlog $2.36B (+137% YoY).
- Landmark agreement to acquire Iridium (design/build/launch/operate own constellations).
- >$437M in new launch contracts (Electron, HASTE, Neutron) in Q2 + post-quarter;
  launch backlog 90+ missions (company high).
- Introduced GHOST globally-deployable launch system; first location LC-4 at Pacific
  Spaceport Complex, Kodiak, Alaska; operational debut suborbital 2027.
- Neutron: critical AIT milestones; Stage 1 tank production aligned with target delivery
  of Neutron to the launch pad in Q4 2026.
- $397M contract (incl. options) for multiple Flatellite spacecraft on Neutron for
  U.S. Space Force SB-AMTI (Space-Based Airborne Moving Target Indicator); one of two
  vendors delivering launch-plus-spacecraft.
- >$160M across two contracts to build three GEO satellites, including Space Force SSC
  prime for two space-domain-awareness satellites (first GEO sat production/ops for USG).
- Formally established Rocket Lab Germany GmbH.

Q3 2026 GUIDANCE
- Revenue: $250–265M
- GAAP Gross Margins: 29–31%
- Non-GAAP Gross Margins: 35–37%
- GAAP Operating Expenses: $143–149M
- Non-GAAP Operating Expenses: $121–127M
- Interest Income, net: $21M
- Adjusted EBITDA loss: $17–23M
- Basic WASO: 641M (incl. ~41M Series A convertible preferred)
- SBC expected $18–20M in Q3
