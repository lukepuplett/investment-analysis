HEADLINE
Rocket Lab Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results: Posts Record Revenue,
$1.2 Billion or 52% Sequential Quarterly Backlog Growth, Positive GAAP Operating
Cash Flow, and Guides to Record Revenue and Gross Margin Expansion in Q4 2026
Long Beach, California — November 9, 2026
================================================================================

CEO (Sir Peter Beck) key quotes:
- Record $268 million in revenue ($34 million higher than Q2 2026) alongside a
  record backlog which has grown to $3.57 billion.
- Nearly $1 billion of that backlog comes from new launch contracts, signed in
  Q3 2026 alone, as well as $470 million signed for Space Systems.

HIGHLIGHTS (Q3 2026 + post-period updates cited in PR)
- Record quarterly revenue of $268 million, a 73% increase year-on-year.
  (Income statement: total revenues $267.834 million vs $155.080 million in Q3 2025.)
- Record backlog of approximately $3.6 billion, a 52% increase since end of Q2 2026.
- Positive GAAP operating cash flow of $7.2 million.
- Approximately $1 billion in new launch contracts signed across Electron, HASTE,
  and Neutron, bringing total launch backlog to 117 launches (a new record).
- Secured over $515 million in Neutron launch contracts from constellation
  operators in Q3, growing the rocket’s backlog to 16 missions ahead of its debut
  and lifting the base launch price to $70 million for several of these missions
  as industry launch capacity tightens. First launch now targeted for no earlier
  than 2027; critical milestones continue across assembly, integration, testing,
  and delivery of first flight hardware to Launch Complex 3.
- Twelve Rocket Lab-built spacecraft successfully launched to orbit and now
  operating (eight Globalstar platforms; twin NASA Mars science spacecraft;
  USSF VICTUS HAZE; one Viasat NASA Communications Services Depot spacecraft).
- More than 55 spacecraft now in development and build (NASA, SDA, USSF, and
  commercial constellation operators).
- Fully funded the pending acquisition of Iridium Communications through a
  completed ATM equity offering; Iridium lenders approved maintaining Iridium’s
  credit facility post-transaction; Iridium shareholders voted overwhelmingly
  in favor of the acquisition.
