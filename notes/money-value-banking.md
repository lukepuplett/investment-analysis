# Money, value and banking: a short explainer

*Written 2026-10-15 from a conversation about "money as the AI bottleneck".*

## Value vs money
- **Value** is real output: useful work done, goods made, problems solved more cheaply.
- **Money** is a claim on future output. A **loan** is a bet that the output will turn up to repay it.
- Credit can pull future output forward; it cannot create it.

## How money is created
- **Banks create money when they lend.** A $1bn loan to CoreWeave creates a $1bn deposit. CoreWeave pays Nvidia, and the deposit moves to Nvidia's bank. In aggregate it never "runs out".
- **Repaying a loan destroys money.** The money supply grows only when new lending outpaces repayment.
- **The central bank steers, it doesn't ration.** It sets the price of reserves (the policy rate) to keep lending, and so inflation, near target. Banks are constrained by capital, risk appetite and rates, not by a pile of deposits.
- **Other channels:** government deficits and QE also create deposits, but they exist to stabilise the economy, not to match growth.

## Keeping money in step with new value
1. More output means bigger incomes and assets to borrow against, so more lending and more deposits.
2. The central bank cuts or raises rates to speed lending up or slow it down.
3. **Velocity:** the same money can change hands faster.
4. Prices can fall. That is fine for real wages (the late 1800s had growth alongside deflation), but it raises the real burden of debt, which is fixed in money. That's why central banks lean against deflation.

## "Money is the bottleneck" (AI build-out)
- It really means **willing lenders are the bottleneck**: someone has to take the risk, at a price the borrower can afford. That comes down to trust and time.
- **What eases it:** proven revenue (take-or-pay contracts with strong counterparties, AI users who pay), cheaper risk (lower rates or term premium, guarantors), and equity or retained profits (hyperscaler capex funded from their own cash flow).
- **What doesn't ease it:** more liquidity without returns. That just pushes up prices and rates, which tightens the bottleneck.
- **The loop:** value creation (AI diffusion, margins) is what makes financing flow. It is the cause, not the result.
