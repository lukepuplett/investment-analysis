- At 30 Jun 2026, cash and cash equivalents were $112.4B and short-term investments were $19.3B, a total of $131,651M. Total capital raised was $141.1B (Prospectus Summary).
- Note 13 shows non-cancelable purchase commitments (CSP hosting and infrastructure) of $54,559M as of 31 Dec 2025. By year: 2026 $7,059M; 2027 $11,128M; 2028 $10,832M; 2029 $8,304M; 2030 $5,428M; thereafter $11,808M.

- The commitments cover summary and the MD&A Contractual Obligations table, which run "as of and subsequent to Sep 30, 2026", are left **blank** in this S-1. NRR definition: Net Revenue Retention is the Current Period revenue of Qualified Customers divided by their Base Period revenue. A Qualified Customer is one with at least $1 of revenue in the Base Period, excluding Claude Pro and Max subscribers. The Base Period is the three months ending one year before the measurement date. The Current Period is the three months ending on the measurement date. Because the base threshold is $1, small customers that grew a great deal can push the figure up sharply.

## Verdict on "Anthropic is generating cash. They're profitable."

The claim is partly true. It depends on the period and on which measure of profit is used.
- **Cash:** For the first time, operating cash flow was positive in H1 2026, at $3.9B. After $0.5B of capex, free cash flow was about $3.4B. Every full year before that burned cash: FCF was about –$1.6B in 2023, –$5.7B in 2024 and –$2.8B in 2025. Part of the H1 2026 cash came from consuming compute that had been prepaid in earlier years (+$2.1B), plus higher payables and deferred revenue. Because compute is leased as a service rather than bought as capex, the "cash flow after compute" is already inside OCF.
- **Profit:** GAAP operating income turned positive in only one quarter (Q2 2026, $974M). For H1 2026 as a whole, there was a GAAP operating loss of $693M. Adjusted (non-GAAP) operating income was $1.6B. Net loss was $80.7B, driven mainly by non-cash fair-value charges on convertible instruments.
- **Forward obligations:** At least $265.6B of fixed compute commitments were outstanding at mid-2026, against $131.7B of cash and investments. The full commitments figure, including deals after 30 Sep 2026, has not yet been filled in.

Saying "Anthropic generated cash in H1 2026 and was operating-profitable in Q2 2026" is supported by the filing. Saying "they're profitable" without qualification is not: the filing shows a GAAP operating loss for H1 2026 and a very large net loss. The filing does not directly address whether tokens are subsidized. However, compute and infrastructure cost was 48% of revenue in Q2 2026, and implied gross margin on the functional basis was about 85%. Both point to positive unit economics at the gross level.
