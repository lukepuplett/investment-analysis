## Numbers ($m unless stated, Q3-26 vs Q3-25)
- Net sales **201,944 vs 180,169 (+12%; +13% ex-FX)**; guide $197–202bn, so at the top. Prime Day moved into Q2 this year; Amazon says growth would be ~400bp higher ex the shift.
- Operating income **28,076 vs 17,422 (+61%)**, margin 13.9% ("highest operating margin ever"); guide $22.5–26.5bn, so a clear beat.
- **AWS 45,984 (+39%, fastest in nearly five years; $184bn run rate)**; AWS OI 17,773 (38.7% margin vs 34.6% a year ago, 39.4% in Q2).
- North America 112,852 (+6%), OI 8,723 (7.7% margin); International 43,108 (+5%, +8% ex-FX), OI 1,580.
- Advertising 19,637 (+12% ex-FX, vs +26% in Q2; Amazon cites Prime Day timing and expects high-teens growth in Q4). Subscriptions 14,006 (+12% ex-FX).
- Net income 20,685 vs 21,187; **EPS $1.89 vs $1.95**. Prior year included $9.5bn pre-tax Anthropic gains in non-operating income; Q3-26 other income was (703).
- **Cash:** Q3 OCF 42,422; purchases of P&E (77,259), proceeds 1,381, so Q3 FCF ≈ **(33.5)bn**. **TTM FCF (42,357)** vs +14,788 a year ago; TTM net capex 210,657 (+82%).
- Balance sheet: long-term debt 173,125 vs 65,648 at Dec-25; other assets 358,775 vs 122,607 (likely the Anthropic/OpenAI-type investments; confirm in the 10-Q).

## Q4 2026 guidance
- Net sales $240–247bn (+12% to 16%); operating income $28.5–33.5bn (vs $25.0bn Q4-25).
- 2026 cash capex still "approximately $220 billion".
- Jassy: AWS projected growth "over 40% both in 2027 and in 2028".

## Delta vs thesis (`analysis/2026_09_delta_analysis.md`, 8/10)
- AWS-growth and AWS-margin pillars both validated again (39% growth, ~39% margin); retail margins at records.
- The weak spot is cash: FCF deeply negative on AI capex and LT debt up ~$107bn YTD. That's the bet working as designed so far, but it raises the bar for AWS returns.
- **Thesis unchanged; nothing invalidating.** Hold/trim is Luke's call.
