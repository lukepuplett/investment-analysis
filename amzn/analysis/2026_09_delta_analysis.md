## Executive Summary

**BLUF: Amazon has materially outperformed expectations since January 2026. AWS re-accelerated from 20% to 37% YoY growth (highest in years), operating income expanded from $68.6B to $93.7B TTM (+37%), and revenue grew to $778B TTM (+18% YoY). However, the capital intensity story has intensified: CapEx guidance increased to ~$200B for FY2026 (vs $125B in FY2025), driving TTM FCF negative at $(7.6)B. Net income of $135.3B is heavily distorted by $70.2B in non-cash Anthropic MTM gains — use Operating Income ($93.7B) for valuation. At $256.78 ($2.77T market cap), the stock trades at 3.6x P/S and ~30x TTM Operating Income. Conviction upgraded to 8/10 from 7/10 on AWS execution.**
