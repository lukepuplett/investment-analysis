## BLUF
Operations are slightly better, balance sheet is worse, and the funding actions after the print are the main news. Liquidity of $184M against a guided $(80)–(90)M Q4 free cash outflow gives about two quarters of runway, and going-concern doubt is repeated. Thesis (first commercial flights in 2027) is intact on schedule but financing risk dominates; the existing "going concern, heavy dilution" view is reinforced.

## Schedule
Apeiron first glide flight expected January 2027; first commercial spaceflight early Q2 2027; second ship late Q2/early Q3 2027; 150 flights/year capacity by end-2028 (new hangar 2H 2028). Limited expedition seats opened at $850k.

## Post-print financing (new since the figures file)
- **12 Nov:** new prospectus supplement for an at-the-market programme of up to **$250M** with Jefferies, on top of the $145M gross raised under ATM in the first nine months.
- **13 Nov:** exchange agreement for about **$12.5M** of the 2.50% 2027 convertible notes into shares; remaining 2027 notes fall from $17.9M to $5.4M.
- **13 Nov:** First Lien Notes (9.80%, due 2028) indenture amended with holder consent to let the company sell certain assets without mandatory redemption of the notes (asset sales are being contemplated; no consent fee).

## Read
Dilution continues. Full use of the $250M ATM at about the current price would add very roughly 90M shares, which is more than half the existing count. Treat that as arithmetic, not a forecast of usage.
