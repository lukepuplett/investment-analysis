## Executive Summary

**BLUF: Virgin Galactic's fundamental situation has deteriorated significantly since October 2025. Cash has declined from $508M to $286M, shares outstanding have increased 4.6x (33M → 152M), and the stock has fallen from $3.91 to $2.92 (-25%). However, the investment thesis pivot point approaches: October 2026 flight test and February 2027 commercial launch remain on track, with $750K ticket pricing validated by oversubscription. Going concern risk remains elevated with ~$100-110M YE 2026 cash implied. This is now a binary outcome situation.**

- **Recommendation:** Speculative Hold for high-risk investors; position sizing critical
