- TTM net loss of $(264)M represents improvement from FY2024 $(347)M

- Interest expense increasing due to new 9.80% first-lien notes

### EPS Dilution Impact
- Basic EPS improved from $(13.89) to $(2.15) despite higher losses
- Massive share count increase (33M → 152M) distorts per-share metrics
- Share count increased 4.6x from FY2024 to current

### Key Observations
1. Revenue remains minimal until commercial operations begin (Feb 2027)
2. Operating expenses have been substantially reduced
3. Higher interest burden from debt restructuring
4. Path to profitability depends entirely on commercial launch execution
