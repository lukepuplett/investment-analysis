## Operational update and guidance (Ex. 99.1)

- First glide flight of lead spaceship Apeiron expected in January 2027; first commercial spaceflight expected early Q2 2027.
- Second spaceship expected to begin commercial spaceflight in late Q2 / early Q3 2027.
- A new high-cadence launch hangar and fueling infrastructure, planned to open in 2H 2028, is expected to raise capacity by 20% to 150 spaceflights a year.
- Rocket-motor production facility construction at the Arizona Spaceship Factory is complete, with final occupancy approval pending.
- **Q4 2026 free cash flow guidance: $(80) million to $(90) million.**

**Cash runway:** $184 million of headline liquidity at 30 September, against guided Q4 FCF of $(80)–$(90) million, implies about $94–104 million at year-end if the guide is met and there is no new financing. At the Q3 burn rate of $99 million a quarter, that is roughly two quarters of cash from 30 September — through Q4 and most of Q1 2027 — which runs out around the first commercial flight, now planned for early Q2 2027. Restricted cash ($31 million) is inside the $184 million headline and may not all be spendable. The 10-Q's substantial-doubt language is consistent with this: more equity (or other) issuance is likely before ticket revenue arrives.
