### Correction of the 12 November evening recap

The first-pass AMAT multiples used a ~$296B TheStreet market cap and were "about 30x trailing, about 22x run-rate and about 8.6x sales."

- **Trailing ~30x and run-rate ~22x are confirmed**, because those are price / EPS and do not depend on the share count: 30.2x GAAP and 29.6x non-GAAP trailing; 21.9x on the $4.35 Q1 midpoint annualised.
- **The market cap and P/S need a correction.** $380.95 × the filed 798 million Q4 diluted WAS is **$304.0B**, not $296B. Trailing P/S on that cap is **8.83x**, not 8.6x. TheStreet's ~$296B implies about 777 million shares, which is below the 793.6 million shares outstanding already reported at Q3-end, even before adding back Q4 issuances or subtracting the $2.00B Q4 buyback. Until the 10-K gives a year-end count, 8.83x on diluted WAS (or ~8.8x on the July outstanding count) is the better figure. Net cash means EV/sales (8.74x) is slightly tighter than P/S.
