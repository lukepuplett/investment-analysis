# AMAT — fiscal Q4 FY26 delta (light note)

**Held.** 23 Nov 2026. Sources: 8-K Item 2.02 of 12 Nov 2026 (acc. 0001628280-26-073360, EX-99.1) and the 26 Oct Item 8.01 8-K (Besi stake). Sourced figures and valuation: `amat/quarterly/2026_Q4_figures.md`. The FY26 10-K had not been located on EDGAR as of my 23 Nov check, so segment data below is from the release.

## BLUF
Record quarter, ahead of consensus (revenue $10.41B vs ~$10.30B; non-GAAP EPS $4.16 vs ~$4.05, press-sourced) with Q1 guide above consensus ($11.1B ±$0.5B vs ~$10.89B). Semiconductor Systems is on track to grow more than 40% in calendar 2026 (company). Thesis intact; the AI/memory-driven cycle is accelerating. Cyclical-peak risk is the thing to hold in mind, not a break in the thesis.

## Outlook
Q1 FY27: revenue $11.1B ±$0.5B; non-GAAP EPS $4.35 ±$0.20 (includes a $0.04 tax benefit). No full-year guide.
