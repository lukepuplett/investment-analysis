## What changed

### Ninth Circuit (28 Aug 2026) — adverse on sports event contracts
- **Case:** KalshiEX (and related relief requests involving Robinhood / Crypto.com) vs Nevada Gaming Control Board path; opinion affirming dissolution of injunction as to **sports** event contracts.
- **Holding (core):** sports-related event contracts are **not** “swaps” under the CEA in the Ninth Circuit’s view; Nevada may treat them as sports betting subject to state gaming law. Election contracts remanded separately.
- **Opinion PDF:** https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2026/08/28/25-7516.pdf
