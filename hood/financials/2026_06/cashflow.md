## Key Notes
- **New $2.2B convertible note issuance** is the standout financing event this half — first debt on the balance sheet since IPO. Worth understanding conversion terms/dilution potential for the next analysis refresh.
- **RVI (Robinhood Ventures Inc.) IPO'd in the quarter**, raising $312M and triggering deconsolidation (a $106M non-cash gain flowed through the income statement's "Other income, net"). This is a one-time item — should be excluded when assessing run-rate profitability.
- Robinhood continued share buybacks ($664M in H1 2026 vs. $446M H1 2025) even while issuing new convertible debt — an aggressive capital allocation posture worth flagging (funding buybacks with debt proceeds, in effect).
- Credit Card Funding Trust activity (purchases/collections of credit card receivables) is scaling meaningfully (~4x YoY on purchases) — reflects the Robinhood credit card product's growing balance sheet footprint.
