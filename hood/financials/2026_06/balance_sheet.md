## Key Notes
- **Long-term borrowings of $2,170M is new this half** — reflects the $2.2B convertible senior notes issued in H1 2026 (see cash flow statement); Robinhood was debt-free at 12/31/2025.
- Accumulated deficit shrank from ($2,152)M to ($1,241)M, i.e. Robinhood earned back $911M of its historical losses in six months — consistent with H1 net income attributable to Robinhood of $911M and no dividends paid.
- Balance sheet growth (total assets +48% in six months) is largely driven by brokerage/clearing mechanics (receivables from users, securities borrowed/loaned) scaling with trading activity and margin balances, not organic asset accumulation — normal for a brokerage business model, but means "total assets" is not a clean proxy for company size/value here.
