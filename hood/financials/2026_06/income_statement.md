## Notable Items
- **Other income, net** jumped to $135M in Q2 2026 (from $3M in Q2 2025), driven largely by a **$106M gain on deconsolidation of RVI** (Robinhood Ventures Inc. — see cash flow notes; RVI completed an IPO in the quarter, raising $312M, after which Robinhood no longer consolidates it).
- Provision for income taxes roughly doubled YoY on a smaller pre-tax income increase — effective tax rate rose from ~12.7% (Q2 2025) to ~19.2% (Q2 2026). Worth flagging as a margin headwind to watch.
- Transaction-based/other revenue growth (+45.4% YoY in Q2) is significantly outpacing net interest revenue growth (+9.0%) — the mix continues shifting toward trading activity vs. NII (cash/margin lending economics).
