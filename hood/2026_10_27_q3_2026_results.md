# Robinhood (HOOD) Q3 2026 results

Released Tuesday 27 October 2026 at 16:05 ET (20:05 GMT), for the quarter ended 30 September 2026. Written 27 October 2026, about 22:10 GMT.

## Summary

Revenue reached a record $1.42bn, up 12% year on year. That was in line with LSEG consensus according to CNBC, and slightly below the $1.43bn consensus cited by Investing.com. GAAP diluted EPS fell 16% to $0.51, which missed the 56 cent LSEG consensus (CNBC). The quarter included a $67m ($0.07 per share) unrealised mark-to-market loss, mainly on Robinhood Ventures Fund I. Robinhood introduced a new adjusted EPS measure this quarter; adjusted EPS was $0.60, down 3%, which beat the $0.55 estimate cited by Investing.com. Crypto revenue fell 55%. Event contracts revenue grew more than sixfold to $173m and options revenue rose 31%. Transaction revenue of $867m was below the $879m StreetAccount estimate (CNBC).

The shares closed the regular session at $94.56. In after-hours trading they were at $87.30 at 22:05 GMT, down 7.7% from the close, according to the Yahoo chart API.

## Versus consensus

- **Revenue:** $1.42bn against $1.42bn LSEG consensus, in line (CNBC). Investing.com cites $1.43bn, which would make it a slight miss.
- **EPS:** GAAP $0.51 against $0.56 LSEG consensus, a miss (CNBC). Adjusted $0.60 against $0.55, a beat (Investing.com).
- **Transaction revenue:** $867m against the $879m StreetAccount estimate, a miss (CNBC).

## Guidance

Robinhood narrowed its 2026 outlook for adjusted operating expenses plus SBC to $2.675-2.75bn, from $2.675-2.775bn. This lowers the top end by $25m and reflects efficiency gains. No revenue guidance was given.

## Notable points

- **Prediction markets:** Event contracts traded rose more than sevenfold to a record 16.4bn. Rothera, the joint venture with Susquehanna, passed $100m in annualised revenue in its first full quarter. Earnings contracts were added to the prediction markets hub.
- **Crypto weakness:** Crypto notional volume fell 38% to $50bn, and Robinhood app crypto volume fell 48%. Crypto trading launched in the UK. Robinhood also received DFSA authorisation in Dubai, with a rollout planned for 2027. Robinhood Chain generated more than $80bn of DEX volume in its first quarter on mainnet.
- **Q4 momentum:** October net deposits were already above $13bn month-to-date. That includes $6bn from the Dell family's contribution to Trump Accounts.
- **AI:** About 200k customers have opened Agentic Trading Accounts, generating $1.6bn of notional volume. Robinhood Agents and 24/7 trading of select US equities were announced at HOOD Summit, along with US perpetual futures.
- **Banking and credit:** Robinhood Banking holds nearly $5bn of deposits. The Gold Card has 1.3m customers. Credit loss provisions are rising as the card book grows. The margin book reached a record $22.1bn, up 59%.
