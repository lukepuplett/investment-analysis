## Numbers ($m unless stated, Q3-26 vs Q3-25; quarter ended 26 Sep 2026)
- Revenue **16,513 vs 13,653 (+21%)**. Guide was $15.8–16.8bn, so upper half.
- Gross margin GAAP 43.0% (vs 38.2%); **non-GAAP 44.1%** (vs 40.0%; guide 42.0%).
- Operating income GAAP 2,522 vs 683 (15.3% margin vs 5.0%); non-GAAP 3,234 (19.6%).
- EPS GAAP $0.60 vs $0.90 (prior year had a $5.45bn Altera divestiture gain); **non-GAAP $0.52 vs $0.23** (guide $0.38). GAAP Q3 includes a $1,005m mark-to-market gain on the DOC Escrowed Shares.
- Segments: CCPG 8,097 (−5%), OI 2,285; **DCAI 7,318 (+78%)**, OI 3,039 (vs 964); Intel Foundry 6,697 (+58%, mostly intersegment), **OI (1,853) vs (2,321)**; All Other 692 (−30%, Altera deconsolidated).
- Cash: OCF 5,082 (vs 2,546); gross capex 4,219; **adjusted FCF 1,639** (vs 896). Cash + ST investments 54.0bn (21,846 + 32,159) vs 37.4bn at Dec-25; debt 46,317 LT + 3,817 ST.
- **Dilution:** completed a $23bn public equity offering (9M net proceeds 22,617). Diluted shares 5,307m vs 4,531m (+17% y/y).
- 9M GAAP net loss (11,609), driven by 12,614 of Escrowed Shares mark-to-market losses and 3,969 restructuring/other (non-cash / non-operating).
- Headcount 81.1k (vs 88.4k).

## Q4 2026 guidance
- Revenue $15.0–16.0bn (midpoint down ~6% q/q); **non-GAAP GM 38.0%** (GAAP 37.1%); non-GAAP EPS $0.25 (GAAP $0.10).
- CFO: PC slowdown expected to continue "at least through the first half of next year"; "disciplined investments in capacity and working capital".
