## Key Notes
- **Capex ($6.2B H1) is down 29% YoY** from $8.7B — a meaningful deceleration in fab spending pace, worth checking against Intel's stated foundry buildout targets for signs of a pullback vs. planned pacing.
- **$14.3B "partner distributions" outflow** is the standout financing item — this appears tied to the Q2 buyout of Apollo's 49% Ireland SCIP minority stake for ~$14.2B (disclosed in the income statement notes), consolidating full Intel ownership of that manufacturing JV.
- New term debt issuance ($13.0B) alongside $9.0B of repayment — net debt increase of ~$4B, funding the SCIP buyout and continued capex amid reduced government incentive proceeds ($167M H1 2026 vs. $964M H1 2025).
- Operating cash flow strength (+$8.1B H1, more than double YoY) is a genuinely positive, real signal underneath the confusing GAAP net loss headline.
