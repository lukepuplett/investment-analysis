## Key Notes
- **Total assets fell $9.0B and total equity fell $23.2B** in six months — the equity decline is much larger than the asset decline because it reflects both the H1 net loss (largely the non-cash Escrowed Shares mark-to-market, see Income Statement notes) and a $14.3B "partner distribution" cash outflow (see Cash Flow statement) related to Intel's manufacturing joint-venture/partner structures (e.g. Ireland SCIP — Intel reacquired Apollo's 49% minority stake in Ireland SCIP for ~$14.2B cash in Q2, consolidating full ownership).
- **Other accrued liabilities rose from $15.2B to $21.2B** and **other long-term liabilities rose from $9.4B to $15.1B** — combined, these lines carry the ballooning Escrowed Shares derivative liability (fair value $15.6B at 6/27/2026 vs. $2.7B at 12/27/2025).
- **Debt (long-term) rose from $44.1B to $48.5B** despite $9.0B of term debt repayment, because $13.0B of new term debt was issued in H1 — net debt is increasing to fund capex and the Ireland SCIP buyout.
- Cash + short-term investments fell from $37.4B to $29.7B — a meaningful liquidity drawdown this half, consistent with heavy capex and the $14.2B SCIP acquisition.

*All values in millions USD unless noted.*
