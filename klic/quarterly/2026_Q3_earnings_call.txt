## About Kulicke and Soffa Industries

Kulicke and Soffa Industries (NASDAQ:KLIC), Ltd. develops and manufactures equipment and tools used in semiconductor and electronic assembly. Its solutions support the packaging, interconnection and assembly of integrated circuits, light-emitting diodes and other electronic components.

The company’s product portfolio includes wire bonding systems, wedge bonders, die attach equipment, advanced packaging systems and related consumables. Its technologies are used in applications such as automotive electronics, consumer electronics, communications, computing, power semiconductors and optoelectronics. Kulicke and Soffa also provides equipment for advanced display and semiconductor packaging applications.

Kulicke and Soffa traces its history to the Kulicke Company, founded in 1951, and has developed into a global supplier serving semiconductor and electronics manufacturers across Asia, North America, Europe and other markets. The company is headquartered in Singapore, with additional operations and customer support activities in major semiconductor manufacturing regions. Its common stock trades on the Nasdaq under the symbol KLIC.
