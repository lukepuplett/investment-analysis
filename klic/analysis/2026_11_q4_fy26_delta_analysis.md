## Mix: advanced packaging / TCB
- FY26 segment revenue (10-K): Ball Bonding $768.0M (64.9% of total, +162%), Wedge $102.3M (8.6%, −7.5%), **Advanced Solutions $112.7M (9.5%, +55%)**, APS $151.0M (12.8%), Other $48.9M.
- Advanced Solutions is where TCB/die-attach sits. Growth was driven by AI/data-centre and heterogeneous packaging. Its segment gross margin fell to 44.4% (from 58.0%) on mix, and the segment made an operating loss in FY26 (about −$38M, against +$49M in FY25 when it included a $71M project-cancellation reimbursement).
- So the FY26 uplift was mostly **core ball-bonding (wire bond) volume**, not TCB. The Q3 call guided TCB to beat $100M this year and $150–200M in FY27; the FY26 split by product line was not disclosed in the filings I read, so I can't confirm TCB landed at or above $100M.
- CEO Talluri (new in the role) says core wire-bond demand is "resilient", and that Advanced Solutions system manufacturing started in the new Singapore production floor in fiscal Q1 FY27.
- **Backlog $720.3M** at 3 Oct 2026, against $245.3M a year earlier (about 2.9x); this supports the Q1 guide.
