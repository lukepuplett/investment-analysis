## Executive Summary & BLUF

**BLUF: KLIC remains a valid **layer-1** bottleneck play (advanced assembly capacity for AI/data-center semis) with improving **layer-2** capture (49%→48% gross margins, TCB scale), but **layer-3** already reflected much of the ramp (stock off ~17% from post–Q2 highs despite Q3 beat). Q3 FY2026 confirms revenue power ($330M, guide raise to $375M Q4) and **TCB FY27 $150–200M**, while **China OSAT receivables concentration** and **YTD cash conversion** remain the gating risks. **Recommendation: HOLD** for existing holders; **do not trim** into Nov earnings unless Q4 guide is cut or AR/OCF diverge again. **Add** only on evidence of sustained OCF >15% of revenue and stable GM ≥48% through FY2027 guide.**
