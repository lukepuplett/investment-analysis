## Strategic / Technology Delta (TCB, hybrid, HBM)

1. **TCB:** Management reiterates fluxless thermal compression as the **production solution for current heterogeneous integration**, while **investing in hybrid bonding** for a later commercial window. FY27 revenue target **$150–200M** for TCB alone (up from implicit ~$120M straight-line math).
2. **Capacity:** Singapore Advanced Solutions expansion **on track H1 FY2027**; traditional wire-bond capacity cited as **4×** over two quarters.
3. **HBM:** No new “volume HBM” milestone in Q3 filing language; opportunity remains **OSAT/foundry/IDM engagements** and **FLEX-ES TCB** for logic-side heterogeneous integration. **Vertical wire** for cost-sensitive stacked DRAM still in development/customer work.
4. **Hybrid bonder:** Explicit Q3 call mention of **panel-level and hybrid bonding platforms** — competitive response to foundry hybrid roadmaps; revenue timing still **out-year**.
