# Broadcom Inc. (AVGO)

**Held?** Yes — confirmed held interest **2026-09-18** (Luke).  
**Last refresh:** 2026-09-28  
**Price context:** ~$353 (25 Sep 2026); ~$1.7T market cap; 52-week ~$290–$495 (market data).

## BLUF

**HOLD core — add on AI-scare pullbacks; trim only if customer-concentration + multiple re-rate together.**

Broadcom sits on the **AI data-center bottleneck stack**: custom AI accelerators (hyperscaler ASICs) plus merchant **networking silicon** (Tomahawk/Jericho class switches), with **VMware** providing recurring infrastructure-software cash flow post-acquisition. Q3 FY2026 (ended 2 Aug 2026) was a record: **$29.6B** revenue (+86% YoY), **$13.7B** FCF (~46% of revenue), and **~$16.7B** AI semiconductor revenue (+221% YoY). Q4 guide **~$34.8B** revenue with AI semi outlook **~$21.7B**.

The stock trades **far below the 2026 peak (~$495)** despite earnings power that management and sell-side frame as still accelerating — classic **Layer 3** tension: fundamentals (Layer 1) and capture (Layer 2) look strong, but **hyperscaler concentration**, **China/export noise**, and **“already priced AI”** episodes create drawdowns.

**Stance for Luke (held):** **HOLD** the strategic AI-infrastructure sleeve weight; **add** on dislocations that leave Sep-2026 AI revenue trajectory intact (e.g. post-earnings selloffs without guide cuts); **trim** only if valuation re-expands toward prior highs *without* upward revision to AI/custom backlog, or if top-customer mix breaches red-flag thresholds (see risk doc).

## Near-term catalysts (6–12 months)

1. **Q4 FY2026 results** (Nov 2026 fiscal year-end) vs **~$34.8B** revenue guide  
2. Hyperscaler capex / custom ASIC deployment commentary (top customers drive >40–55% of revenue — monitor 10-Q risk disclosures)  
3. VMware Cloud Foundation / subscription renewal cycles and any large deal churn  
4. China / export-control headlines on networking gear (sector volatility Sep 2026)  
5. Debt paydown pace vs FCF (post-VMware acquisition overhang)

## Key risks

- **Customer concentration** — handful of hyperscalers dominate AI semi + networking orders  
- **Custom silicon share loss** if customers insource more or dual-source  
- **Merchant networking competition** (Nvidia Spectrum-X, Marvell, others) in AI fabrics  
- **VMware integration / renewal** — software growth slower than AI semi; churn or pricing pressure  
- **Leverage** — still **~$57B** long-term debt; must keep FCF high through cycles  
- **Multiple compression** — AI trade de-rating can hit AVGO even when guides rise
