## What the price implies (Layer 3)

In one sentence: **the market pays for ~2–3 years of very high AI semi growth but discounts durability beyond that and penalizes concentration/leverage.**

- Sep 2026 tape (~$353) sits **below** the ~$495 52-week high despite Q3 records — implies skepticism on **duration**, not **current** prints.  
- Post-earnings (Sep 2) guide raise on AI semi was not enough to restore peak multiple — **“show me FY2027”** regime.
