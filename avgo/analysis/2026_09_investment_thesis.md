# AVGO — Investment thesis (Sep 2026)

## Executive Summary

**BLUF: Broadcom is a held name; stance is HOLD. Layer 1 (AI data-center bottleneck: custom silicon + networking) and Layer 2 (value capture via hyperscaler co-design + VMware switching costs) remain intact after Q3 FY2026 records, but Layer 3 requires patience at ~$353 because the market already ran the AI re-rate once and now debates duration and customer concentration.**

## One-liner

Broadcom monetizes the AI infrastructure build-out twice: **merchant and custom silicon** in the cluster, plus **recurring VMware cash** — with FCF that de-levers the balance sheet if growth persists.

## THINKING_FRAMEWORK.md — Layer 1: Fundamentals (bottleneck)

| Checklist item | Answer |
|----------------|--------|
| **MISTER C inputs / outputs** | Takes **information** (designs, firmware, VMware stack), **matter/energy** (fabs/OSAT, power-hungry clusters), **CI&A** (hyperscaler architect attention) → outputs **AI compute + network fabric + enterprise cloud foundation**. |
| **Current bottleneck?** | **Yes — AI data-center scale-out**: custom accelerators and high-speed Ethernet fabrics are supply-constrained, evidenced by **+221% YoY AI semi revenue** and **+86% consolidated revenue** in Q3 FY2026. |
| **Evidence** | Q3 revenue **$29.6B**; AI semi **~$16.7B**; Q4 AI semi outlook **~$21.7B**; OCF **$14.2B** (company filings). |
| **Shelf life / what ends it** | **3–5 years** of intense build-out, then risk of **supply catch-up**, **customer insourcing**, and bottleneck shift to **power/grid** (see THINKING_FRAMEWORK NVDA/ASML examples). |

## Layer 2: Value capture (Helmer / moat)

| Checklist item | Answer |
|----------------|--------|
| **Who owns the scarce input?** | Broadcom **owns/co-develops** ASIC designs and networking IP; VMware **owns** virtualization installed base — not a commodity reseller. |
| **Who fights for profit?** | **Hyperscalers** (price/volume), **employees/SBC**, **debtholders**, **regulators/China** — not fragmented consumers. |
| **Helmer powers** | **Cornered resource** (customer-specific ASIC programs); **switching costs** (VMware + qual’d networking); **scale economies** (semi R&D spread); **process power** (Hock Tan integration cadence). |
| **ROIC vs WACC** | Reported margins and FCF suggest **ROIC > WACC** at peak AI cycle — stress-test in a semi down-year (see risk doc). |
| **Commoditisation** | Merchant networking faces **Nvidia verticalization**; custom ASICs face **insourcing** — innovation may pass value to cloud customers over time. |
| **Share count / dilution** | ~**4.774B** shares (MRQ) — watch SBC; FCF currently overwhelms dilution concern. |
| **Cycle timing** | **Mid-build-out**: supplier capturing abnormal profit; market question is **late-cycle vs early-cycle extension** into FY2027–28. |

| Checklist item | Answer |

## Inversion checklist (how this goes wrong)

| Failure mode | AVGO-specific |
|--------------|---------------|
| Right bottleneck, wrong captor | Hyperscalers keep capex but **insource ASICs** — AVGO grows slower than AI spend. |
| Stale bottleneck | Power/grid limits clusters; **networking/ASIC orders plateau** while stock still priced for growth. |
| Narrative ≠ bottleneck | “AI stock” label without **order backlog** — guide cuts on Sep-style earnings. |
| Rule-maker | **Export controls / China reviews** of networking gear (Sep 2026 volatility). |
| Death by dilution | Less likely near-term given FCF; watch **large M&A** or SBC step-ups. |
| Paying twice for Layer 1 | Re-rating back to **$495+** without **FY2027 AI revenue uplift** — trim case. |
| Rationalisation | Treating VMware as “safe” while **renewals slip** — software line decelerates faster than modeled. |
