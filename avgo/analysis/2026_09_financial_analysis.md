## Quality of earnings

- **Non-GAAP gap** is material (SBC, acquisition-related costs). Operational trend aligns with non-GAAP op income (+92% YoY Q3), but GAAP is the right anchor for leverage ratios.  
- **FCF conversion** is excellent in Q3 (46% of revenue) — thesis-positive for dividends and debt paydown.  
- **Customer concentration** (see 10-Q risk factors) means revenue quality is “ lumpy but large ” — one capex pause at a top hyperscaler moves the consolidated line.
