**Held.** 23 Nov 2026. Sources: 8-K Item 2.02 of 12 Nov 2026 (acc. 0000858877-26-000170), 10-Q filed 17 Nov (acc. 0000858877-26-000185, not reconciled). Full sourced figures and valuation: `csco/quarterly/2027_Q1_figures.md`. Prior: `csco/analysis/2026_08_*`.

## BLUF
Strongest growth in 15+ years and a guide raise. Revenue, non-GAAP operating margin and EPS all beat the top of guidance. The August watch item, gross-margin compression from AI hardware and memory costs, is playing out as guided, with operating leverage more than offsetting it.

## Guidance (raised)
- **AI infrastructure orders/revenue from hyperscalers: $9B in FY27**, more than double FY26 (raised).
- FY27 revenue **$74.8–75.8B** (was $72.2–73.4B); non-GAAP EPS **$5.47–5.53** (was $5.05–5.11).
- Q2: revenue $18.0–18.2B, non-GAAP EPS $1.34–1.36, non-GAAP GM 65–66%, op margin 36–37%.

## Read against Aug thesis
- Demand pillar strongly validated; the "networking supercycle" framing now comes from management.
- Margin: product GM 63.7% non-GAAP (−350bp YoY). Mix-driven, guided, offset by opex (non-GAAP opex +2% on revenue +24%).
- Security at +7% is the weak spot (August flagged one-off Splunk on-prem benefit in Q4); services +1%.
- RPO growth (+8%) is well below revenue growth (+24%), i.e. the revenue surge is shorter-cycle hardware.
- Insider Form 4s and a 144 flurry 12–20 Nov are noted but not analysed.
